# On the Determination of the State Border Between the Republic of Latvia and the Republic of Belarus

The *Saeima* <sup>1</sup> has adopted and
the President has proclaimed the following law:

On the Determination of the State Border Between the Republic of Latvia and the Republic of Belarus

**Section 1.** This Law hereby adopts and approves the Agreement on Determining the State Border Between the Republic of Latvia and the Republic of Belarus (hereinafter - the Agreement) signed on 21 February 1994 in Minsk.

**Section 2.** The Law shall come into force on the day of its proclamation. By this Law shall be proclaimed the Agreement referred to in Section 1 of this Law and the maps appended thereto.

**Section 3.** The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Latvia shall, on the basis of this Law and in accordance with Article 8 of the Agreement, prepare the instruments of ratification for exchange with the Republic of Belarus.

**Section 4.** The Agreement shall come into effect in accordance with the procedures determined in Article 8 thereof.

The Law has been adopted by the *Saeima* on 10 November 1994.

Acting for the President,
Chairperson of the *Saeima* A. Gorbunovs

Adopted 12 November 1994

<sup>1</sup> The Parliament of the Republic of Latvia
