**Section 7.** The composition of educational, cultural and scientific objects of national significance shall be determined by the Minister for Finance on the basis of the application from the relevant ministry and shall be submitted for approval to the Cabinet within two months from the day of coming into force of this Law.

[*17 February 1997*]

**Section 8.** If an educational, cultural and scientific institution of national significance is reorganised, the institutions which take over the rights and obligations of the aforementioned institutions shall keep the status of an educational, cultural and scientific object of national significance.

**Section 9.** The Cabinet has the right to liquidate educational, cultural and scientific institutions of national significance by preserving the status of an educational, cultural and scientific object of national significance for the relevant institution in the following cases:

1) the object is taken over by another educational, cultural and scientific institution of national significance for the performance of its functions;

2) the state establishes and finances a new and only educational, cultural and scientific institution at the national level for the further use of the object.

The Law shall come into force on the day following its proclamation.

The Law has been adopted by the *Saeima* on 26 October 1995.
