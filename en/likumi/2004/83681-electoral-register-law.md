The *Saeima*<sup>1</sup> has adopted and
the President has proclaimed the following law:

**Section 1.** The purpose of this Law is to ensure the establishment of a unified voter registration system in the territory of the Republic of Latvia, i.e. the Electoral Register (hereinafter - the Register), and a system for registering participation of voters in elections, i.e. the Electronic Online Electoral Register (hereinafter - the Online Register), and the processing of the data entered therein.

[*20 June 2024*]

**Section 2.** The Register shall ensure the recording of the persons who, in accordance with the Election to the European Parliament Law, the Law on the Election of Local Government Councils, the Law on the Election of the *Saeima*, and the Local Government Referendum Law, have the right to vote in the Republic of Latvia (hereinafter - the voters).

[*6 December 2023* / *The new wording of Section shall come into force on 1 August 2025.* *See Paragraph 2 of Transitional Provisions*]

**Section 2.<sup>1</sup>** The Online Register shall ensure the verification of the right to vote of voters and making of a record on the participation of a voter in the election to the European Parliament, the election of local government councils, or the election of the *Saeima* (hereinafter - the elections) or on the participation of a voter in a local government referendum (hereinafter - the local government referendum).

[*20 June 2024* / *The Section in respect of the use of the Register and the Online Register in the local government referendum shall be applied from 1 August 2025.* *See Paragraph 4 of Transitional Provisions*]

**Section 3.** (1) The Central Election Commission shall implement the methodological management and supervision of the operation of the Register and the Online Register.

(2) The information entered in the Register shall be processed and the Register shall be managed by the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs (hereinafter - the Office).

(3) The information entered in the Online Register shall be processed and the Online Register shall be managed by the State Regional Development Agency (hereinafter - the Agency).

[*20 June 2024*]

**Section 4.** (1) Expenditures related to the establishment and maintenance of the Register and the Online Register shall be covered from the State budget.

(2) The Register shall be placed and maintained within the infrastructure of the Information Centre of the Ministry of the Interior by the Information Centre of the Ministry of the Interior. The Online Register shall be placed within the infrastructure of *valsts akciju sabiedrība "Latvijas Valsts radio un televīzijas centrs"* [State joint-stock company Latvian Radio and Television Centre] and maintained by the State joint-stock company Latvian Radio and Television Centre.

[*20 June 2024*]

**Section 5.** (1) Information on the Latvian citizens who have reached 18 years of age on the day of the elections and are registered in the Register of Natural Persons shall be entered, updated, and stored in the Register.

(1<sup>1</sup>) Information on such citizens of the European Union who are not Latvian citizens but are registered in the Register of Natural Persons and who have reached 18 years of age on the day of the election of local government councils shall be entered, updated, and stored in the Register.

(2) Information on citizens of the European Union who are not Latvian citizens and have reached 18 years of age on the day of the election to the European Parliament, who are registered in the Register of Natural Persons or have applied to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and on whom the Central Election Commission has taken the decision to include them in the Register in accordance with the law shall be entered, updated, and stored in the Register.

(3) Information on the Latvian citizens and citizens of the European Union who are not Latvian citizens but are registered in the Register of Natural Persons and who, on the day of adoption of the decision on holding the local government referendum, have reached 18 years of age and whose place of residence is registered within the administrative territory of the respective local government, or who own an immovable property which is registered in the administrative territory of the respective local government in accordance with the procedures laid down in law shall be entered, updated, and stored in the Register.

(4) The electoral roll and the record on the participation of a voter in the elections and the local government referendum shall be entered, updated, and stored in the Online Register.

[*30 September 2004; 11 May 2006; 17 July 2008; 20 December 2018; 11 February 2021; 23 September 2021; 6 December 2023; 20 June 2024* / *Paragraph three shall come into force on 1 August 2025.* *See Paragraphs 2, 3 and 4 of Transitional Provisions*]

**Section 6.** (1) The following shall be entered and updated in the Register and the Online Register:

1) the following information shall be initially included in the Register from the Register of Natural Persons:

a) given name (names);

b) surname;

c) date of birth;

d) citizenship;

e) address of the declared, registered, or indicated place of residence;

f) address for determining the constituency;

g) information on the personal identification document;

h) personal identity number;

2) the following information shall be entered and updated during the operation of the Register:

a) given name (names);

b) surname;

c) date of birth;

d) citizenship;

e) address of the declared, registered, or indicated place of residence;

f) address for determining the constituency;

g) personal identity number or voter code;

h) constituency;

i) record that the voter has applied to vote by post in the election to the European Parliament and that the voting materials are available to the voter via the electronic service available on the portal of State administration services;

j) the electronic mail address to which the address of the website where the voting materials are available is to be sent if the voter has applied to vote by post in the election of the *Saeima* or the election of local government councils;

k) record that the address of the website where the voting materials are available is to be sent to the voter's official electronic address if the voter has applied to vote by post in the election of the *Saeima* or the election of local government councils;

l) foreign country from which the voter plans to send his or her vote if he or she has applied to vote by post;

m) sequential number for voting by post;

n) indication that the person owns an immovable property which is registered in the administrative territory of the relevant local government in accordance with the procedures laid down in law;

o) information on the entry in or removal from the Register;

p) name of the elections or the local government referendum;

3) the following information shall be initially included in the Online Register from the Register:

a) given name (names);

b) surname;

c) personal identity number or voter code;

d) constituency;

e) indication that the voter has applied to vote by post;

f) foreign country from which the voter plans to send his or her vote if he or she has applied to vote by post;

g) sequential number for voting by post;

h) personal identification document number processed using the hash function;

i) information identifying the electoral procedure or local government referendum;

4) information provided by the Central Election Commission on each polling station established for the elections or the local government referendum (number, address, name, working hours) and the operating mode thereof (casting a vote for depositing thereof, advance voting, voting on the day of election or referendum), and also on employees of polling stations (given name, surname, personal identity number, e-mail address, information on the assignment to a polling station or an election commission) for whom a user account has to be created in the Online Register shall be initially entered in the Online Register;

5) the following information shall be entered and updated during the operation of the Online Register:

a) information on the polling station where the voter has voted (number), the date and time of registration of the voter's vote;

b) record on voting using a registration envelope;

c) record on the cancellation of participation.

(2) When entering information in the Register or the Online Register or updating it, the date of and reason for the entry or update shall be specified.

(3) Within the meaning of this Law, the election of the *Saeima* shall take place in one constituency.

(4) In the elections or the local government referendum, information on a voter shall be included in the electoral roll of the Register, adding a record on the constituency to which the voter is assigned. The electoral roll shall be created electronically.

[*30 September 2004; 17 July 2008; 26 February 2009; 20 December 2018; 11 June 2020; 11 February 2021; 23 September 2021; 6 December 2023; 20 June 2024* / *The new wording of Paragraph one and Paragraph four in respect of the use of the Register and the Online Register in the local government referendum shall be applied from 1 August 2025.* *See Paragraph 4 of Transitional Provisions*]

**Section 7.** The Central Election Commission shall, 50 days before the elections, submit to the Office and the Agency information on all polling stations established, specifying the constituency, number, name, and address of each polling station, and also inform the Office and the Agency within three days of any changes in this information.

[*11 June 2020; 23 September 2021; 20 June 2024*]

**Section 7.<sup>1</sup>** The election commission of a respective local government shall inform the Office and the Agency of the date, name of the local government referendum and the administrative territory of the local government in which the local government referendum will take place within one working day after adoption of the decision on holding a local government referendum but not later than by 12:00 on the nineteenth working day before the day the local government referendum takes place.

[*6 December 2023; 20 June 2024* / *Section shall come into force from 1 August 2025.* *See Paragraphs 2 and 3 of Transitional Provisions*]

**Section 8.** [11 June 2020]

**Section 9.** (1) Based on the information in the Register of Natural Persons, the Office shall enter in the Register information on Latvian citizens who will have reached 18 years of age on the day of regular elections or the day of adoption of the decision on holding a local government referendum:

1) 90 days before the election of local government councils;

2) 45 days before the election of the *Saeima* or the election to the European Parliament;

3) not earlier than 19 working days before the day the local government referendum takes place.

(2) Based on the information in the Register of Natural Persons, the Office shall, 90 days before the election of local government councils, enter information into the Register on such citizens of the European Union who are not Latvian citizens but are registered in the Register of Natural Persons and who will reach 18 years of age on the day of the regular elections.

(3) Based on the information in the Register of Natural Persons, the Office shall, not earlier than 19 days before the day the local government referendum takes place, enter information in the Register on such citizens of the European Union who are not Latvian citizens but are registered in the Register of Natural Persons and who, on the day of adoption of the decision on holding a local government referendum, will have reached 18 years of age.

[*30 September 2004; 17 July 2008; 20 December 2018; 11 February 2021; 23 September 2021; 6 December 2023* / *Clause 3 of Paragraph one and Paragraph three shall come into force on 1 August 2025.* *See Paragraph 2 of Transitional Provisions*]

**Section 10.** The Central Election Commission shall, not later than 20 days before the election to the European Parliament, enter information into the Register on the citizens of the European Union who are not Latvian citizens but who are registered in the Register of Natural Persons and on whom the Central Election Commission has taken the decision to enter them in the Register in accordance with the procedures laid down in law.

[*23 September 2021*]

**Section 11.** The Central Election Commission shall, not later than 20 days before the election to the European Parliament, enter information into the Register on the citizens of the European Union who, in accordance with the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations of 18 April 1961 and the Vienna Convention on Consular Relations of 24 April 1963, are exempt from registration in the Register of Natural Persons and who have applied to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs to vote in the election to the European Parliament in the Republic of Latvia not later than 30 days before the election.

[*23 September 2021*]

**Section 11.<sup>1</sup>** [11 June 2020]

**Section 12.** (1) The Office shall prepare the initial electoral roll:

1) not later than 75 days before the election of local government councils for each constituency;

2) not later than 35 days before the election of the *Saeima* or the election to the European Parliament;

3) not later than 15 working days before the day when the local government referendum takes place for the constituency in which the local government referendum will take place.

(2) The electoral roll of the constituency shall include all the voters who have declared their place of residence at the addresses belonging to the relevant constituency or whose last registered address of the place of residence was at the addresses belonging to such constituency.

(3) In the election of local government councils, the voters who have registered their place of residence in a foreign country shall be included in the electoral roll of voters living abroad.

(4) For the election of local government councils or for the local government referendum, only those voters whose registered place of residence is at an address within the respective constituency shall be included in the electoral roll of the constituency.

(5) If the election of local government councils take place concurrently with the election to the European Parliament or election of the *Saeima*, the voters who have the right to participate in both elections shall be included in the electoral roll of the constituency at the addresses belonging to which the place of residence of the voters is registered in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Election of Local Government Councils.

[*30 September 2004; 17 July 2008; 26 February 2009; 11 June 2020; 11 February 2021; 23 September 2021; 6 December 2023; 20 June 2024* / *Clause 3 of Paragraph one shall come into force on 1 August 2025.* *See Paragraph 2 of Transitional Provisions*]

**Section 12.<sup>1</sup>** The Office shall, not later than at 10:00 on the sixth day before the election or the local government referendum, hand over the electoral roll to the Online Register with the necessary information on the persons with the right to vote who have the right to participate in the elections or the local government referendum. The Office shall hand over the electoral roll to the Agency in a coordinated, secure manner.

[*20 June 2024* / *The Section in respect of the use of the Register and the Online Register in the local government referendum shall be applied from 1 August 2025.* *See Paragraph 4 of Transitional Provisions*]

**Section 13.** (1) The Office shall, on the 75<sup>th</sup> and 14<sup>th</sup> day before the election of local government councils, send information on the possibility to ascertain the constituency to those voters who have an activated official electronic address account.

(2) The information referred to in Paragraph one of this Section shall not be sent to the voters who do not have an activated official electronic address account.

(3) The Office shall, not later than 70 days before the election of local government councils, provide the voter with a possibility to receive information free of charge on the constituency in the electoral roll of which he or she has been included by using the electronic service available on the website of the Office.

[*11 June 2020; 23 September 2021*]

**Section 14.** (1) The voter shall vote in the constituency in the electoral roll of which he or she has been included in accordance with the information entered in the Online Register.

(2) The voter has the right to change the constituency for the local government council elections by choosing another constituency in which he or she has an immovable property registered in accordance with the procedures laid down in law or in which he or she has declared his or her place of residence.

(3) [11 June 2020]

(4) In respect of his or her wish to change the constituency in the cases referred to in Paragraph two of this Section the voter shall, not earlier than 70 days and not later than 9 days before the elections, submit a submission in person to any local government or its institution for the declaration of the place of residence. Persons on whom arrest is imposed as a security measure or who are serving a sentence related to the deprivation of liberty shall submit the submission for the change of the constituency to the prison administration.

(4<sup>1</sup>) The voter may also change his or her constituency free of charge electronically not earlier than 70 days and not later than 9 days before the elections by using the electronic service available on the website of the Office.

(5) If the voter chooses a constituency for the election of local government councils in which he or she has an immovable property registered in accordance with the procedures laid down in law, he or she shall in addition present to the local government or its institution for the declaration of a place of residence a document certifying his or her property rights.

(6) A local government or its institution for the declaration of the place of residence or the prison administration shall, using online data transmission, update the information entered in the Register on the change of the constituency on the day of receipt of the submission.

(7) [30 September 2004]

[*30 September 2004; 11 May 2006; 17 July 2008; 26 February 2009; 9 February 2017; 20 December 2018; 11 June 2020; 11 February 2021; 20 June 2024*]

**Section 15.** (1) If a submission of a citizen of the European Union has been received with a request to cancel the Register entry in respect of him or her or if the citizen of the European Union has lost the right to vote in the Member State of the European Union of which he or she is a citizen, the Central Election Commission shall remove the relevant information from the Register.

(2) The Office shall, in accordance with the information available in the Register of Natural Person, enter information into the Register and update it in the following cases:

1) a voter has died;

2) [20 December 2018];

3) a voter has acquired or lost Latvian citizenship;

4) a voter has changed his or her given name (names) or surname;

5) a voter has changed the personal identification document;

6) a voter has lost citizenship of the European Union;

7) the legal ground for the registration of a voter in the Register of Natural Persons has been lost;

8) the voter has changed his or her personal identity number.

(3) The Prison Administration shall, according to the information entered into the Prisoner Information System in conformity with the relevant election law, update the information entered into the Register if a voter is serving a sentence at a prison.

(3<sup>1</sup>) The Court Administration shall provide the Office with the following information from the State Unified Computerised Land Register on the persons who, on the day of adoption of the decision on holding the local government referendum, own an immovable property registered in accordance with the procedures laid down in law and located within the administrative territory of the local government in which the local government referendum will take place:

1) personal identity number;

2) administrative territory;

3) classification code of territorial units.

(4) In the election to the European Parliament, the election of local government councils, and the election of the *Saeima*, the information entered in the Register in accordance with Paragraphs one, two, and three of this Section shall be updated by 18:00 on the seventh day before the elections.

(5) Information entered in the Register for the respective constituency for the local government referendum shall be updated in accordance with Paragraphs one, two, and 3.<sup>1</sup> of this Section not later than by 18:00 on the seventh day before the day when the local government referendum takes place.

(6) After updating in the Register the information referred to in Paragraphs four and five of this Section, the Office shall hand over the prepared electoral roll to the Agency for entry in the Online Register in accordance with the procedures laid down in Section 12.<sup>1</sup> of this Law.

[*30 September 2004; 26 February 2009; 9 February 2017; 20 December 2018; 11 June 2020; 23 September 2021; 6 December 2023; 20 June 2024* / *Paragraphs 3.<sup>1</sup> and five shall come into force on 1 August 2025.* *See Paragraphs 2 and 3 of Transitional Provisions*]

**Section 16.** [11 June 2020]

**Section 17.** If a revote takes place in separate polling stations, the election commission shall, 12 days before the revote, send information to the voter that he or she has been included in the electoral roll of revote. The official electronic address of the voter shall be used to send information, but if such address does not exist, the address of the declared, registered, or indicated place of residence in Latvia shall be used. The Agency shall, not later than 24 days before the revote, ensure that the information from the Online Register on the voters who have voted in advance, and also the information on the voters who have voted in advance and who, on the date of compilation of the roll, have an active official electronic address account, is handed over to the election commission.

[*20 June 2024*]

**Section 18.** After receipt of an application for voting by post, the commission of the polling station for voting by post or the Central Election Commission shall make a record in the Register in the online data transmission mode on the application of the voter to participate in the relevant election and vote by post and shall indicate the address of the voter in the foreign country to which the documents for voting by post are to be sent or the electronic mail address or official electronic address of the voter to which the address of the website where the voting materials are available is to be sent.

[*6 December 2023*]

**Section 19.** (1) [20 June 2024]

(1<sup>1</sup>) In the election to the European Parliament, the election of local government councils, and the election of the *Saeima*, or the local government referendum, the Agency shall, not later than at 23:00 on the sixth day before the day of the elections or the local government referendum, ensure that the Online Register is available to the polling station commissions.

(2) [11 June 2020]

(3) [11 June 2020]

[*30 September 2004; 11 June 2020; 20 June 2024* / *Paragraph 1.<sup>1</sup> in respect of the use of the Register and the Online Register in the local government referendum shall be applied from 1 August 2025.* *See Paragraph 4 of Transitional Provisions*]

**Section 20.** (1) The information entered in the Register and the Online Register shall be deleted on the 30<sup>th</sup> day after the expiry of the time limit for contesting the results of the elections or the local government referendum. In case the results of the elections or the local government referendum are contested or appealed, the information in the Register and the Online Register shall be destroyed on the 30<sup>th</sup> day after entry into effect of the final ruling.

(2) The Central Election Commission and the Office have to right to process the information entered into the Register for statistical purposes.

(3) The Central Election Commission and the Agency have the right to process the information included in the Online Register for statistical purposes.

[*23 September 2021; 20 June 2024* / *Paragraph three in respect of the use of the Register and the Online Register in the local government referendum shall be applied from 1 August 2025.* *See Paragraph 4 of Transitional Provisions*]

**Section 21.** The decision on the entry of a person in the Register, refusal to enter him or her in the Register or his or her removal from it may be appealed against before a court in accordance with the procedures laid down in the Administrative Procedure Law. The court shall examine an application not later than within three working days from the initiation of the case. The court judgment shall be enforced without delay.

[*11 May 2006*]

## Informative Reference to European Union Directive

[*11 May 2006*]

The Law contains legal norms arising from Council Directive 93/109/EC of 6 December 1993 laying down detailed arrangements for the exercise of the right to vote and stand as a candidate in elections to the European Parliament for citizens of the Union residing in a Member State of which they are not nationals.

The Law shall come into force on 5 February 2004.
