# Law On Convention on the European Forest Institute

Only the latter is authentic. The original Latvian text uses masculine pronouns in the singular. The Translation and Terminology Centre uses the principle of gender-neutral language in its English translations. In addition, gender-specific Latvian nouns have been translated as gender-neutral terms, e.g. *chairperson*.

The *Saeima* has adopted
and the President has proclaimed the following Law:

## Section 1.

The Convention on the European Forest Institute of 28 August 2003 (hereinafter -the Convention) is adopted and approved with this Law.

## Section 2.

The fulfilment of obligations provided for in the Convention shall be co-ordinated by the Ministry of Agriculture.

## Section 3.

The Convention shall come into force in accordance with the period of time and procedures specified in Section 15 thereof, and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall notify thereof in the newspaper *Latvijas Vēstnesis* [the official Gazette of the Government of the Republic of Latvia].

## Section 4.

This Law shall come into force on the date of its proclamation. Together with this Law the Convention in English and the translation thereof into Latvian shall be proclaimed.

This Law has been adopted by the *Saeima* on 19 April 2007.

Acting for the President, Chairperson of the *Saeima* I. Emsis

Rīga, 5 May 2007
