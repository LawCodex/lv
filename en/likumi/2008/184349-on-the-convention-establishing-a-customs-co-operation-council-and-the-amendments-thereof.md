# On the Convention Establishing a Customs Co-operation Council and the Amendments Thereof

Disclaimer: The English language text below is provided by the State Language Centre for information only; it confers no rights and imposes no obligations separate from those conferred or imposed by the legislation formally adopted and published. Only the latter is authentic. The original Latvian text uses masculine pronouns in the singular. The State Language Centre uses the principle of gender-neutral language in its English translations. In addition, gender-specific Latvian nouns have been translated as gender-neutral terms, e.g. *chairperson*.

The *Saeima*<sup>1</sup> has adopted
and the President has proclaimed the following Law:

**Section 1.** The Convention Establishing a Customs Co-operation Council of 15 December 1950 (hereinafter - Convention) and the Recommendation of the Customs Co-operation Council of 30 June 2007 concerning the amendments of the Convention Establishing a Customs Co-operation Council (hereinafter - Amendments) are adopted and approved with this Law.

**Section 2.** The fulfilment of obligations provided for in the Convention and the Amendments shall be co-ordinated by the Ministry of Finance.

**Section 3.** It shall be recognised that, according to the instrument of accession to the Convention of the Council of Ministers of 8 May 1992 and Article 18 of the Convention, the Convention shall apply in Latvia from 22 June 1992.

**Section 4.** The Amendments shall come into force in accordance with the period of time and procedures specified in Article 20 of the Convention, and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall notify thereof in the newspaper *Latvijas Vēstnesis* [the official Gazette of the Government of the Republic of Latvia].

**Section 5.** This Law shall come into force on the day following the proclamation thereof. Along with the Law the Convention and the Amendments shall be proclaimed in English with a translation into Latvian thereof.

This Law has been adopted by the *Saeima* on 13 November 2008.

President V. Zatlers

Riga, 27 November 2008

<sup>1</sup> The Parliament of the Republic of Latvia
