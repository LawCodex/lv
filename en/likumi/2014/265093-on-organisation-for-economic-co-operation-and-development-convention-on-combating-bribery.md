# On Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development Convention on Combating Bribery of Foreign Public Officials in International Business Transactions

The *Saeima*<sup>1</sup> has adopted and
the President has proclaimed the following Law:

On Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development Convention on Combating Bribery of Foreign Public Officials in International Business Transactions

**Section 1.** Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development Convention on Combating Bribery of Foreign Officials in International Business Transactions (hereinafter - the Convention) of 21 November 1997 is accepted and approved by this Law.

**Section 2.** In accordance with Article 4, Paragraph 3 of the Convention the General Prosecutor's Office shall be the competent authority for advice on jurisdictional issues in pre-trial proceedings, and the Ministry of Justice - during the trial or after judgment enters into effect.

**Section 3.** In accordance with Article 9 of the Convention the General Prosecutor's Office shall be the competent authority for sending and receipt of mutual assistance requests during pre-trial proceedings, but the Ministry of Justice - after a case is brought to the court.

**Section 4.** In accordance with Article 10 of the Convention the General Prosecutor's Office shall be the competent authority for sending and receipt of requests for extradition of a person.

**Section 5**. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall notify the Secretary-General of the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development on the competent authorities referred to in Sections 2, 3 and 4 of this Law.

**Section 6.** The Convention shall come into force within the time period and in accordance with procedures stipulated in Article 13, Paragraph 2 thereof, and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall notify thereof in the official gazette *Latvijas Vēstnesis* [the official Gazette of the Government of the Republic of Latvia].

**Section 7.** This Law shall come into force on the day following the proclamation thereof. By this Law the Convention in English and the translation thereof into Latvian shall be proclaimed.

The *Saeima* adopted this Law on 6 March 2014.

President of Latvia A. Bērziņš

Rīga, 20 March 2014

<sup>1</sup> The Parliament of the Republic of Latvia
