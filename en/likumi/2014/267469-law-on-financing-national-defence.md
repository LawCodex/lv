# Law on Financing National Defence

Text consolidated by Valsts valodas centrs (State Language Centre) with amending laws of:

21 April 2022 [shall come into force on 23 April 2022];
7 December 2023 [shall come into force on 1 January 2024];
26 March 2026 [shall come into force on 28 March 2026].

If a whole or part of a section has been amended, the date of the amending law appears in square brackets at the end of the section. If a whole section, paragraph or clause has been deleted, the date of the deletion appears in square brackets beside the deleted section, paragraph or clause.

The *Saeima*<sup>1</sup> has adopted and
the President has proclaimed the following law:

**Section 1.** The purpose of the Law is to purposefully strengthen national defence capacities through financial methods for Latvia to fulfil its obligations within the scope of collective defence as a Member State of the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation, and also to pave the way towards implementation of long-term development projects of national defence and thus to support the involvement of Latvia in European and transatlantic security bodies.

**Section 2.** (1) In compliance with the conditions of the Treaty on Stability, Coordination and Governance in the Economic and Monetary Union signed on 2 March 2012 and the objective of increasing the national defence financing in 2020, 2021, and 2022 to at least 2 per cent of the amount of the forecasted gross domestic product, and also taking into account the geopolitical changes that have taken place in relation to Russia's full-scale military invasion of Ukraine on 24 February 2022 and the objective to accelerate the development of the National Armed Forces by ensuring national defence financing in 2027 in the amount of at least 5 per cent of the amount of the forecasted gross domestic product, the Cabinet shall draw up the draft annual State budget law and the draft medium term budget framework law by allocating financing to national defence:

1) in 2015 - at least 1.0 per cent of the amount of the gross domestic product forecasted for the respective year;

2) in 2016 - at least 1.1 per cent of the amount of the gross domestic product forecasted for the respective year;

3) in 2017 - at least 1.3 per cent of the amount of the gross domestic product forecasted for the respective year;

4) in 2018 - at least 1.5 per cent of the amount of the gross domestic product forecasted for the respective year;

5) in 2019 - at least 1.75 per cent of the amount of the gross domestic product forecasted for the respective year;

6) from 2020 to 2022 - at least 2 per cent of the amount of the gross domestic product forecasted for the respective year;

7) in 2023 - 2.25 per cent of the amount of the gross domestic product forecasted for the respective year, but not less than EUR 886 466 480;

8) in 2024 - 2.4 per cent of the amount of the gross domestic product forecasted for the respective year, but not less than EUR 1 002 485 480;

9) in 2025 - 2.5 per cent of the amount of the gross domestic product forecasted for the respective year, but not less than EUR 1 103 916 480;

10) in 2026 - at least 2.75 per cent of the amount of the gross domestic product forecasted for the respective year;

11) in 2027 and subsequent years - at least 5 per cent of the amount of the gross domestic product forecasted for the respective year.

(2) Based on the situation of national defence, the Cabinet may, during implementation of the State budget, examine the possibilities to reallocate appropriations for increasing defence financing - for long-term development projects (investments) on a priority basis.

[*21 April 2022; 7 December 2023; 26 March 2026*]

## Transitional Provision

With the coming into force of this Law, the Law on Financing National Defence (*Latvijas Republikas Saeimas un Ministru Kabineta Ziņotājs*, 2001, No. 10; 2002, No. 12; 2008, No. 24) is repealed.

The Law has been adopted by the *Saeima* on 3 July 2014.

President A. Bērziņš

Rīga, 10 July 2014
