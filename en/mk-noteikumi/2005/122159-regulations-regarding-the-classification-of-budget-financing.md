# Regulations Regarding the Classification of Budget Financing

Republic of Latvia

**Cabinet
Regulation No. 875**
Adopted 22 November 2005

Issued pursuant to
Section 11, Clause 1 and
Section 43, Paragraph one of the
Law On Budget and Financial Management

1. This Regulation prescribes the classification of budget financing to be applied to the planning, accounting and preparation of reports on the State budget, local government budget, as well as State and local government budget institutions.

2. The classification of budget financing shall determine the net value of transactions according to the types of financial instruments, excluding the fluctuations of the currency exchange rate of the financial instrument, development and revaluation of prices (hereinafter - changes affecting value).

3. Financial transactions involving financial assets and liabilities shall be classified into groups according to the division of financial assets and liabilities in accordance with the system of budget financing classification codes (Annex).

4. In performing financial transactions in foreign currency, recalculations shall be performed according to the relevant currency exchange rate determined by the Bank of Latvia on the day of the transaction.

5. The digit code F00 00 00 00 shall be used in the classification of budget financing. The digits of the classification code shall designate the following indicators:

5.1. the first digit of the classification code shall be the budget financing classification designation "F";

5.2. the second digit of the classification code shall designate the division of transactions into categories of financial instruments:

5.2.1. category F20 01 00 00 "Funds and deposits" shall include the net value of transactions of funds and deposits, which does not include the changes affecting value;

5.2.2. category F30 01 00 00 "Purchased debt securities, except derivative financial instruments" shall include transactions involving debt securities issued by other institutional entities, separating the transactions of purchase and sale, which in total form the net value of transactions which does not include the changes affecting value and the impact of derivative financial instruments;

5.2.3. category F30 02 00 00 "Issued debt securities" shall include transactions with personally issued debt securities, separating the transactions of issuance and maturity, which in total form the net value of transactions which does not include the changes affecting value and the impact of derivative financial instruments;

5.2.4. category F40 02 00 00 "Borrowings" shall include borrowing transactions, separating the transactions for the receipt of borrowings and the repayment thereof, which in total forms the net value of the borrowing transactions which does not include the changes affecting value. Funds received within the scope of a credit line, overdraft and financial leasing shall also be regarded as borrowings;

5.2.5. category F40 01 00 00 "Loans" shall include loan transactions, separating the loans issued and the transactions for repayment of the loans issued, which in total forms the net value of the loan transactions which does not include the changes affecting value;

5.2.6. category F50 01 00 00 "Shares and other participation in the equity capital of merchants" shall include investments into capitals, i.e., the net value of stocks or shares which does not include the changes affecting value;

5.3. the third digit of the classification code shall designate the sub-category division:

5.3.1. sub-category F21 01 00 00 "Funds" shall include the net value of financial transactions;

5.3.2. sub-category F22 01 00 00 "Demand deposits" shall include the net value of all demand deposit transactions, which does not include the changes affecting value. Demand deposits may at any time be converted into foreign currency or transferred, using a cheque, a bank order or a debit entry without any significant restrictions or fines (for example, the balances of funds in settlement accounts in credit institutions);

5.3.3. sub-category F29 01 00 00 "Fixed-term deposits" shall include the net value of the remaining deposit transactions (which are not demand deposits), which does not include the changes affecting value. They shall include fixed-term deposits, saving deposits, deposits according to savings scheme or agreement, short-term sale with repurchase agreements (repo), which are the liabilities of credit institutions and the central bank, the financial claims or liabilities of the Bank of Latvia, which arise from the short-term financing mechanism and the short-term financing mechanism for collateral of funds;

5.3.4. sub-category F55 01 00 00 "Shares and other participation in the equity capital of merchants, not including joint investment fund shares" shall include investment transactions in the equity capital of merchants (including transactions of stocks and shares investments in the capital of merchants), not including joint investment fund shares and separating purchase and sale transactions, which in total forms the net value of transactions which does not include the changes affecting value. Investments in the fixed capital shall be indicated in this group, separating the participation in capitals of related and associated enterprises, investments in capitals of merchants quoted on the stock exchange and not quoted on the stock exchange;

5.3.5. sub-category F56 01 00 00 "Joint investment fund shares" shall include purchase and sale transactions with investment certificates of investment funds of residents or the shares or certificates of investment funds of non-residents, investment trusts and other joint investment schemes (the only aim of which is to invest the attracted funds in the financial or capital market or in immovable property). Purchase and sale transactions in total shall form the net value of transactions, which does not include the changes affecting value;

5.4. the fourth digit of the classification code shall designate the division of maturity according to the initial maturity for assets and liabilities:

5.4.1. (fourth digit 1) "short-term" financial assets and liabilities within the meaning of this Regulation shall include the financial assets and liabilities the initial maturity of which is one year or less;

5.4.2. (fourth digit 2) "medium-term" financial assets and liabilities within the meaning of this Regulation shall include the financial assets and liabilities the initial maturity of which exceeds one year but does not exceed five years;

5.4.3. (fourth digit 3) "long-term" financial assets and liabilities within the meaning of this Regulation shall include the financial assets and liabilities the initial maturity of which exceeds five years;

5.5. the fifth digit of the classification code shall designate the affiliation of the specific assets or liabilities to the asset or liability part of the balance sheet:

5.5.1. (fifth digit 1) "assets of the balance sheet" - shall be applied to transactions the results of which are recorded in the assets part of the balance sheet;

5.5.2. (fifth digit 2) "liabilities of the balance sheet" - shall be applied to transactions the results of which are recorded in the liabilities part of the balance sheet;

5.6. the sixth digit of the classification code shall designate the division of assets and liabilities according to types of interest rates:

5.6.1. (sixth digit 1) "fixed rate" - the interest rate is not linked to any variable indicator in the market, but is determined as invariable throughout the entire maturity of financial asset and liability;

5.6.2. (sixth digit 2) "variable rate" - the interest rate for each instrument includes the financial market index (for example, RIGIBOR, LIBOR, EURIBOR) and a specific number of percentage points;

5.6.3. (sixth digit 3) "interest-free rate" - the interest rate is zero per cent or is subsidised unconditionally;

5.7. the seventh digit of the classification code shall designate the division of financial assets and liabilities according to foreign currency:

5.7.1. (seventh digit 1) financial assets and liabilities in the national currency (in lats);

5.7.2. (seventh digit 2) financial assets and liabilities in euros (in lats);

5.7.3. (seventh digit 3) financial assets and liabilities in the foreign currencies of those states which are not Member States of the Economic and Monetary Union (in lats);

5.8. the eight digit of the classification code shall designate the results of transactions: (eighth digit 1) - increase; (eighth digit 2) - reduction, which in total forms the net value of the transaction;

5.9. the ninth digit of the classification code shall be applied by determining the classification of the sub-category "Shares and other participation in the equity capital of merchants, not including joint investment fund shares":

5.9.1. (ninth digit 1) "Participation in the capital of related enterprises whose shares are quoted on the stock exchange" shall include investment transactions (separating the value of purchase and sale transactions, which does not include the changes affecting value) for financial investments in capitals of related enterprises whose shares are quoted on the stock exchange;

5.9.2. (ninth digit 2) "Participation in the capital of related enterprises whose shares are not quoted on the stock exchange" shall include investment transactions (separating the value of purchase and sale transactions, which do not include the changes affecting value) for financial investments in capitals of related enterprises, whose shares are not quoted on the stock exchange;

5.9.3. (ninth digit 3) "Participation in the capital of related enterprises which are not joint stock companies" shall include the remaining investment transactions (separating the value of purchase and sale transactions, which does not include the changes affecting value) for financial investments in capitals of related enterprises, which are not joint stock companies;

5.9.4. (ninth digit 4) "Participation in the capital of associated enterprises whose shares are quoted on the stock exchange" shall include investment transactions (separating the value of purchase and sale transactions, which does not include the changes affecting value) for financial investments in capitals of associated enterprises, whose shares are quoted on the stock exchange;

5.9.5. (ninth digit 5) "Participation in the capital of associated enterprises whose shares are not quoted on the stock exchange" shall include investment transactions (separating the value of purchase and sale transactions, which does not include the changes affecting value) for financial investments in capitals of associated enterprises, whose shares are not quoted on the stock exchange;

5.9.6. (ninth digit 6) "Participation in the capital of associated enterprises which are not shares" shall include the investment transactions (separating the value of purchase and sale transactions, which does not include the changes affecting value) for financial investments in capitals of associated enterprises, which are not shares;

5.9.7. (ninth digit 7) "Participation in the capital of other merchants, whose shares are quoted on the stock exchange" shall include investment transactions (separating the value of purchase and sale transactions, which does not include the changes affecting value) for financial investments in capitals of merchants, whose shares are quoted on the stock exchange and in which less than 20 per cent of the capital are owned by the State or a local government;

5.9.8. (ninth digit 8) "Participation in the capital of other merchants, whose shares are not quoted on the stock exchange" shall include investment transactions (separating the value of purchase and sale transactions, which does not include the changes affecting value) for financial investments in capitals of merchants, whose shares are not quoted on the stock exchange and in which less than 20 per cent of the capital are owned by the State or a local government; and

5.9.9. (ninth digit 9) "Participation in the capital of other merchants which are not shares" shall include investment transactions (separating the value of purchase and sale transactions, which does not include the changes affecting value) for other financial investments in capitals of merchants, which are not shares and in which less than 20 per cent of the capital are owned by the State or a local government.

6. The procedures by which the classification of budget financing shall be used for the preparation of reports, and maturities, as well as the positions of the mandatory classification for budget planning shall be determined by the regulatory enactments regarding the preparation and submission of reports. The classification of local government budget financing in the budget accounting and in the preparation of reports shall only be applied in such amount as specified in the regulatory enactments regarding the preparation and submission of reports. The classification of local government budget financing shall be used for budget planning at the discretion thereof.

7. Cabinet Regulation No. 91 of 1 February 2005, *Regulations Regarding the Classification of Budget Financing* (*Latvijas Vēstnesis*, 2005, No. 20) is repealed.

8. This Regulation shall come into force on 1 January 2007.

Prime Minister A. Kalvītis

Minister for Finance O. Spurdziņš
