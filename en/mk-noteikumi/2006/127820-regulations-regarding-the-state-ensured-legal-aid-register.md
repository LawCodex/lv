# Regulations Regarding the State Ensured Legal Aid Register

20 July 2010 [shall come into force on 23 July 2010];
1 March 2016 [shall come into force on 8 March 2016];
5 December 2023 [shall come into force on 1 January 2024].

Republic of Latvia

**Cabinet
Regulation No. 107**
Adopted 7 February 2006

Issued pursuant to
Section 28, Paragraph two
of the State Ensured Legal Aid Law

## I. General Provisions

1. The Regulation prescribes the procedures for the setting up of the State Ensured Legal Aid Register (hereinafter - the Register), the scope of and access to the information to be included therein.

2. The Register is a State information system which includes information on:

2.1. the State ensured legal aid (hereinafter - the legal aid) providers, the legal aid recipients and their representatives;

2.2. the decisions taken in legal aid cases and the legal aid granted;

2.3. the monetary funds disbursed to legal aid providers and the monetary funds to be recovered and refunded by a decision of the Court Administration (hereinafter - the Administration).

[*1 March 2016; 5 December 2023*]

3. The Administration shall be the manager and holder of the Register. In managing the funds which are intended for the legal aid, entering into legal aid contracts for this purpose, taking decisions in a legal aid case, disbursing the abovementioned funds, and recovering them in the cases and in accordance with the procedures laid down in laws and regulations, the Administration shall ensure the inclusion of information in the Register, updating, storage, and use of information.

[*1 March 2016*]

4. The Administration is entitled to request and receive, free of charge, the information necessary for the operation of the Register from institutions and the users of the Register.

## II. Information to be Included in the Register

5. The following information shall be included in the Register:

5.1. on the legal aid provider:

5.1.1. the given name (names) and surname or firm name;

5.1.2. the date of birth;

5.1.3. the personal identity number or registration code;

5.1.4. the nationality;

5.1.5. [1 March 2016];

5.1.6. the address of the declared, registered place of residence or of the place of residence indicated by the person;

5.1.7. the address of the place of residence abroad (if any);

5.1.8. the address of the location where the legal aid is provided;

5.1.9. [20 July 2010];

5.1.10. the contact details (telephone, fax number, e-mail address, if any);

5.1.11. the date, number, and period of validity of the legal aid contract;

5.1.12. the competence and the types of legal aid;

5.1.13. the date, number of the complaint received, its substance, and the result of examination;

5.1.14. the date, number, and justification for the decision on the notice of termination of the legal aid contract;

5.1.15. the date and outcome of the decision and ruling taken after contesting or appeal;

5.2. on the legal aid recipient:

5.2.1. the given name (names) and surname;

5.2.2. the date of birth;

5.2.3. the personal identity number;

5.2.4. the address of the declared, registered place of residence or of the place of residence indicated by the person;

5.2.5. the address of the place of residence abroad (if any);

5.2.6. the nationality;

5.2.7. [1 March 2016];

5.2.8. the contact details (if any);

5.2.9. the information on the financial situation (low-income, needy, short description of a specific situation);

5.2.10. [20 July 2010];

5.2.<sup>1</sup> on the representative of the legal aid recipient:

5.2.<sup>1</sup> 1. the given name (names) and surname;

5.2.<sup>1</sup> 2. the date of birth;

5.2.<sup>1</sup> 3. the personal identity number;

5.2.<sup>1</sup> 4. the address of the declared, registered place of residence or of the place of residence indicated by the person;

5.2.<sup>1</sup> 5. the justification for the representation;

5.2.<sup>1</sup> 6. the contact details (if any);

5.3. on the decisions taken in a legal aid case:

5.3.1. the registration date and number of the submission;

5.3.2. the type, date, number, and justification of the decision;

5.3.3. the date and outcome of the decision and ruling taken after contesting or appeal;

5.4. on granting the legal aid:

5.4.1. the type (types), amount of legal aid and the substance of the case;

5.4.2. the date and number of the order;

5.4.3. the data of the assigned legal aid provider (given name, surname, personal identity number or date of birth);

5.4.4. the monetary funds disbursed to the legal aid provider;

5.5. [20 July 2010];

5.6. on the monetary funds to be recovered and refunded by a decision of the Administration:

5.6.1. the type, date, number, and justification of the decision;

5.6.2. the date and outcome of the decision and ruling taken after contesting or appeal;

5.6.3. the amounts of money to be recovered and refunded or a note regarding impossibility of recovery;

5.6.4. the date, number of the warning and execution order;

5.6.5. the enforcement authority.

[*20 July 2010; 1 March 2016 / See Paragraph 20*]

6. Information in the Register shall be included in the Latvian language. If the legal aid provider or recipient is a foreigner, the given name and surname of the person shall be indicated in the original form of the relevant foreign language (from the languages of the Latin alphabet writing system) or in Latin transliteration of the original form (from other languages).

7. The information referred to in Sub-paragraph 5.1 of this Regulation shall be included in the Register on the basis of a submission for the entry into a legal aid contract, the documents appended thereto, and the legal aid contract entered into.

[*1 March 2016*]

8. The information referred to in Sub-paragraphs 5.2 and 5.2.<sup>1</sup> of this Regulation shall be included in the Register on the basis of a submission for the request for legal aid, the documents appended thereto, and the decision taken.

[*1 March 2016*]

9. If the legal aid provider is a natural person, the information referred to in Sub-paragraphs 5.1.1, 5.1.2, 5.1.3, 5.1.4, and 5.1.6 of this Regulation shall be included in the Register using the information included in the Population Register.

[*1 March 2016*]

10. The information referred to in Sub-paragraphs 5.2.1, 5.2.2, 5.2.3, 5.2.4, 5.2.6, 5.2.<sup>1</sup> 1, 5.2.<sup>1</sup> 2, 5.2.<sup>1</sup> 3, and 5.2.<sup>1</sup> 4 of this Regulation shall be included in the Register using the information included in the Population Register.

[*1 March 2016*]

## III. Procedures for the Use of the Information from the Register

11. The information included in the Register may be received by:

11.1. pre-trial investigating institutions, the Offices of the Prosecutor, and judicial authorities - for the conducting of pre-trial criminal proceedings, for the examination and trial of cases;

11.2. sworn bailiffs - for the enforcement of court rulings;

11.3. State and local government institutions - for the carrying out of the tasks specified in laws and regulations;

11.4. persons who need the information for scientific or statistical studies;

11.5. legal aid recipients and legal aid providers - the data applying to them.

12. The Administration shall issue the information from the Register in writing and also using online data transmission.

13. The information issued for the use for scientific or statistical studies shall not contain data identifying persons.

14. In using online data transmission, the information included in the Register shall be issued on the basis of laws and regulations or a written agreement between the Administration and the recipient of the information. It shall include information on the requester of information, the amount of information to be issued, the purpose of the use of information, information on the person requesting the information, and other necessary information.

[*1 March 2016*]

15. In order to receive information, a natural person shall submit a justified submission to the Administration and present a personal identification document. If information is received by a person authorised by the requester of information (natural person), he or she shall present a notarised authorisation and a personal identification document. Upon receipt of information, a representative of a legal person shall present a personal identification document and a document certifying the right of representation of the recipient of information (legal person).

## IV. Information Storage Periods

16. The information included in the Register shall be stored in a database.

[*20 July 2010*]

17. [20 July 2010]

18. The information shall be stored in the Register for 10 years. After expiry of the storage period, the information shall be transferred to the State archives in accordance with the laws and regulations governing the archiving of the documented data existing in the information systems and of electronic documents. The information transferred shall be deleted from the Register, and a relevant deed in relation to it shall be drawn up.

[*20 July 2010*]

## V. Closing Provisions

[*1 March 2016*]

19. Until the development and introduction of the information system of the Register, the Register shall be kept manually and using the existing technological possibilities.

20. The Administration shall include the information referred to in Sub-paragraph 5.6 of this Regulation after adjustment of the Register but not later than from 1 January 2017.

[*1 March 2016*]
