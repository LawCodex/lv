Republic of Latvia

**Cabinet
Regulation No. 460**
Adopted 6 June 2006

Issued pursuant to
Section 6, Paragraph one of the
Law on Regulated Professions and
Recognition of Professional Qualifications

1. These Regulations prescribe the list of specialities and sub-specialities of regulated professions (Annex 1) and the list of additional specialities in the profession of a doctor (Annex 2).

2. Cabinet Regulation No. 337 of 6 August 2002, Regulations Regarding Professions Regulated by Lists of Specialities, Sub-specialities and Additional Specialities (*Latvijas Vēstnesis,* 2002, No. 114) is repealed.

Prime Minister A. Kalvītis

Minister for Education and Science B. Rivža
