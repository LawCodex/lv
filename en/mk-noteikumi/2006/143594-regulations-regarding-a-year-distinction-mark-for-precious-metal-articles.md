# Regulations Regarding a Year Distinction Mark for Precious Metal Articles

Text consolidated by Valsts valodas centrs (State Language Centre) with amending regulations of:

18 July 2017 [shall come into force from 1 September 2017].

If a whole or part of a paragraph has been amended, the date of the amending regulation appears in square brackets at the end of the paragraph. If a whole paragraph or sub-paragraph has been deleted, the date of the deletion appears in square brackets beside the deleted paragraph or sub-paragraph.

Republic of Latvia

**Cabinet
Regulation No. 759**
Adopted 12 September 2006

Issued pursuant to
Section 13, Paragraph one of the law On Supervision of Official Fineness

1. The Regulation prescribes year distinction marks for precious metal articles made in the Republic of Latvia.

2. The following year distinction marks have been specified from 2006 to 2015:

2.1. in 2006 - a Latin letter "N";

2.2. in 2007 - a Latin letter "O";

2.3. in 2008 - a Latin letter "P";

2.4. in 2009 - a Latin letter "R";

2.5. in 2010 - a Latin letter "S";

2.6. in 2011 - a Latin letter "T";

2.7. in 2012 - a Latin letter "U";

2.8. in 2013 - a Latin letter "V";

2.9. in 2014 - a Latin letter "Z";

2.10. in 2015 - a Latin letter "A".

3. The following year distinction marks have been specified from 2017 to 2026:

3.1. in 2017 - a Latin letter "c";

3.2. in 2018 - a combination of Latin letters, a number and graphical symbol "LV1∞" or a Latin letter "d";

3.3. in 2019 - a Latin letter "e";

3.4. in 2020 - a Latin letter "f";

3.5. in 2021 - a Latin letter "g";

3.6. in 2022 - a Latin letter "h";

3.7. in 2023 - a Latin letter "i";

3.8. in 2024 - a Latin letter "j";

3.9. in 2025 - a Latin letter "k";

3.10. in 2026 - a Latin letter "l".

[*18 July 2017*]

Prime Minister A. Kalvītis

Minister for Finance O. Spurdziņš
