Republic of Latvia

**Cabinet
Regulation No. 197**
Adopted 20 March 2007

Issued pursuant to
Section 6, Paragraph three
of the law On State Compensation to Victims

1. The Regulation prescribes the amount of information to be entered in the State Compensation Register (hereinafter - the Register) and the procedures for ensuring access to such information.

2. The following information shall be entered in the Register on a natural person who has been recognised as a victim in accordance with the procedures laid down in the Criminal Procedure Law:

2.1. given name (names), surname;

2.2. personal identity number;

2.3. date of birth;

2.4. nationality;

2.5. address of the place of residence registered in the Republic of Latvia or, if the person does not have a place of residence registered in the Republic of Latvia, address of the actual place of residence (address where the person can be reached);

2.6. contact details (telephone, fax number, e-mail address (if any));

2.7. current account.

[*3 August 2010*]

3. If a person has died as a result of a criminal offence, the following information on the victim shall be entered in the Register:

3.1. given name (names), surname;

3.2. personal identity number;

3.3. date of birth;

3.4. nationality;

3.5. [3 August 2010];

3.6. address of the place of residence registered in the Republic of Latvia or, if the person does not have a place of residence registered in the Republic of Latvia, address of the actual place of residence (address where the person can be reached);

3.7. contact details (telephone, fax number, e-mail address (if any));

3.8. current account.

[*3 August 2010*]

4. If a victim exercises his or her rights with the intermediation of a representative, the following information on the representative shall be entered in the Register:

4.1. given name (names), surname;

4.2. personal identity number;

4.3. date of birth;

4.4. nationality;

4.5. address of the place of residence registered in the Republic of Latvia or, if the person does not have a place of residence registered in the Republic of Latvia, address of the actual place of residence (address where the person can be reached);

4.6. contact details (telephone, fax number, e-mail address (if any));

4.7. current account.

[*3 August 2010*]

5. The following information on a criminal offence shall be entered in the Register:

5.1. time and place of the offence;

5.2. qualification in accordance with the Criminal Law;

5.3. date of commencement of the criminal proceedings and the number of the criminal case;

5.4. nature of the damage caused;

5.5. document indicating the stage of the criminal proceedings at the time of requesting the State compensation (type, number, date of issue, date of entry into effect, issuing country, issuing authority).

[*3 August 2010*]

6. The following information on the decisions taken by the Court Administration (hereinafter - the Administration) and the amount of the compensation disbursed shall be entered in the Register:

6.1. number, date of the decision;

6.2. information on the contesting, appealing of the decision;

6.3. [3 August 2010];

6.4. amount of the compensation disbursed;

6.5. reason for reducing the amount of compensation.

[*5 December 2023*]

7. The following information on the recovery from the victim shall be entered in the Register:

7.1. date and number of the decision to recover the disbursed State compensation;

7.2. amount of the sum to be recovered;

7.3. amount of the sum recovered;

7.4. date when the decision was transferred for enforcement;

7.5. sworn bailiff to whom the decision was transferred for enforcement;

7.6. date of the enforcement or partial enforcement of the decision or a note on the impossibility of recovery.

8. The following information on the recovery from the offender or his or her accomplice shall be entered in the Register:

8.1. given name (names), surname;

8.2. personal identity number;

8.3. date of birth;

8.4. nationality;

8.5. address of the place of residence registered in the Republic of Latvia or, if the person does not have a place of residence registered in the Republic of Latvia, address of the actual place of residence (address where the person can be reached);

8.6. [3 August 2010];

8.7. date and number of such final ruling of the person directing the proceedings which has entered into effect in a criminal case;

8.8. date and number of the decision to recover the disbursed State compensation;

8.9. amount of the sum to be recovered;

8.10. amount of the sum recovered;

8.11. date when the decision was transferred for enforcement;

8.12. sworn bailiff to whom the decision was transferred for enforcement;

8.13. date of the enforcement or partial enforcement of the decision or a note on the impossibility of recovery.

[*3 August 2010*]

9. The information entered in the Register may be received by:

9.1. the person directing the proceedings;

9.2. sworn bailiffs;

9.3. State and local government institutions - for carrying out the tasks specified in laws and regulations;

9.4. persons who need the information for scientific or statistical studies. The information which is issued for use in scientific or statistical studies may not contain personal data;

9.5. the victim - in accordance with the procedures laid down in the laws and regulations which govern the processing and use of the personal data of individuals.

10. The Administration shall issue information containing restricted access information from the Register in written form.

11. In order to receive information from the Register which contains restricted access information, a natural person shall submit a submission to the Administration and present a personal identification document. If information is received by an authorised person of the requester of the information (natural person), he or she shall present a notarised power of attorney and a personal identification document. When receiving information, the representative of a legal person shall present a personal identification document and a document certifying the right of representation of the recipient of information (legal person).

12. If the persons referred to in Sub-paragraphs 9.1, 9.2, and 9.3 of this Regulation need to receive information on a regular basis to carry out the tasks specified in laws and regulations, the information entered in the Register may be issued according to a bilateral contract previously entered into by and between the Administration and the recipient of information, using online data transmission. The requester (authority) of information, the content and amount of the information to be issued, the purpose of use of the information, and information on the person to whom online access to the information is granted shall be determined in the contract.

13. Information shall be stored in the Register for 10 years. After expiry of the storage term, information shall be transferred to the State archives in accordance with the laws and regulations governing the archiving of the recorded data and electronic documents present within the information systems.

14. The electronic information system of the State Compensation Register shall be developed by 1 August 2007. Until the development and introduction of the electronic information system of the Register, the Register shall be kept manually and using the existing technological possibilities.

15. The processes for the processing of the information indicated in Paragraphs 7 and 8 of this Regulation shall be developed and introduced in the electronic information system of the State Compensation Register by 1 January 2014.

[*3 August 2010*]
