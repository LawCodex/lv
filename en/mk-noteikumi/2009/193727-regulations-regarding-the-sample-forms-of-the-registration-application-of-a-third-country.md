# Regulations Regarding the Sample Forms of the Registration Application of a Third Country Auditor or a Third Country Commercial Company of Auditors and the Sample Form of an Opinion on the Conformity of a Third Country Auditor or a Third Country Commercial Company of Auditors to the Conditions for Registration, and the Procedures for the Preparation and Sending of an Opinion

Republic of Latvia

**Cabinet
Regulation No. 537**
Adopted 17 June 2009

[*19 June 2012*]

Issued pursuant to
Section 24.<sup>2</sup>, Paragraphs two and three
of the Law on Audit Services

[*29 October 2024*]

1. The Regulation prescribes the sample forms (Annexes 1, 1.<sup>1</sup>, 1.<sup>2</sup>, and 1.<sup>3</sup>) of the registration application of a third country auditor or a third country commercial company of auditors (hereinafter - the registration application) and the sample form of an opinion of the Ministry of Finance (Annex 2) on the conformity of a third country auditor or a third country commercial company of auditors to the conditions for registration in the Register of Third Country Auditors and Third Country Commercial Companies of Auditors (hereinafter - the opinion) laid down in the Law on Audit Services as well as the procedures for preparing and sending the opinion.

[*29 October 2024*]

2. The Ministry of Finance shall ensure the publication of sample forms of the registration application on the website of the Ministry of Finance in Latvian and English.

[*29 October 2024*]

2.<sup>1</sup> A third country auditor or a third country commercial company of auditors shall fill in and submit the registration application (Annexes 1 and 1.<sup>2</sup>) to the Ministry of Finance if the European Commission has not recognised the supervision, quality control, investigation, and penalty systems of such third country as equivalent to the supervision, quality control, investigation, and penalty systems of the Member States.

[*29 October 2024*]

2.<sup>2</sup> A third country auditor or a third country commercial company of auditors shall fill in and submit the registration application (Annexes 1.<sup>1</sup> and 1.<sup>3</sup>) to the Ministry of Finance if the European Commission has recognised the supervision, quality control, investigation, and penalty systems of such third country as equivalent to the supervision, quality control, investigation, and penalty systems of the Member States.

[*29 October 2024*]

3. The Ministry of Finance shall, within a month after receipt of the registration application (Annexes 1 and 1.<sup>2</sup>), examine the information included in the registration application and:

3.1. if a third country auditor or a third country commercial company of auditors conforms to the conditions for registration in the Register of Third Country Auditors and Third Country Commercial Companies of Auditors, shall prepare a relevant opinion;

3.2. if inaccuracies are established in the received registration application and it is necessary for the preparation of the opinion, shall request an updated registration application or additional information. After receipt of the updated registration application or additional information, the Ministry of Finance shall, within one month, perform the activities determined in Sub-paragraphs 3.1 and 3.3 of this Regulation;

3.3. shall inform the submitter of the registration application in writing of the conformity or non-conformity of a third country auditor or a third country commercial company of auditors to the conditions for registration in the Register of Third Country Auditors and Third Country Commercial Companies of Auditors.

[*29 October 2024*]

3.<sup>1</sup> The Ministry of Finance shall, within two weeks after receipt of the registration application (Annexes 1.<sup>1</sup> and 1.<sup>3</sup>), examine the information included in the registration application and:

3.<sup>1</sup>1. shall prepare a relevant opinion;

3.<sup>1</sup>2. if inaccuracies are established in the received registration application, may request an updated registration application or additional information. After receipt of the updated registration application or additional information, the Ministry of Finance shall, within two weeks, perform the activities determined in Sub-paragraphs 3.<sup>1</sup>1 and 3.<sup>1</sup>3 of this Regulation;

3.<sup>1</sup>3. shall inform the submitter of the registration application in writing of the conformity of a third country auditor or a third country commercial company of auditors to the conditions for registration in the Register of Third Country Auditors and Third Country Commercial Companies of Auditors.

[*29 October 2024*]

4. The opinion together with a copy of the registration application shall be sent by the Ministry of Finance to the Latvian Association of Sworn Auditors.

5. The Regulation shall come into force on 29 June 2009.

Acting for the Prime Minister -
Minister for Finance E. Repše

Minister for Economics A. Kampars
