# Regulations Regarding the Samples of the Local Government Councillor's Certificate and Councillor's Insignia

Republic of Latvia

**Cabinet
Regulation No. 55**
Adopted 19 January 2010

[*15 June 2021*]

Issued pursuant to
Section 18, Paragraph two
of the Law on the Status of the Councillor
of the Local Government Council

[*15 June 2021*]

1. The Regulation specifies the samples of the local government councillor's certificate (hereinafter - the certificate) and the councillor's insignia.

[*15 June 2021*]

2. The certificate is a card with dimensions of 8.5 x 5.4 cm. The sample of the certificate is provided in Annex 1 to this Regulation.

3. The certificate is a document certifying the rights and duties of the official.

4. The certificate shall be deemed invalid if the mandate of the councillor has expired.

5. When a councillor's mandate expires, the certificate shall be returned to the relevant municipality and the municipality shall dispose of the certificate.

[*4 March 2025*]

6. The certificate shall contain:

6.1. the front (obverse) of the certificate:

6.1.1. a photograph of the councillor;

6.1.2. the given name and surname of the councillor;

6.1.3. the title of the councillor's status - "COUNCILLOR";

6.1.4. the name of the relevant local government;

6.1.5. the stamp of the election commission of the relevant local government;

6.1.6. the signature of the chairperson of the election commission of the relevant local government;

6.1.7. the date of issue of the certificate;

6.2. the back (reverse) of the certificate:

6.2.1. the name of the relevant local government;

6.2.2. an image of the local government coat of arms registered in the Register of Coats of Arms or, in the case provided for in the Law On the State Coat of Arms of Latvia, an image of the lesser State coat of arms of the Republic of Latvia.

[*15 June 2021; 4 March 2025*]

6.<sup>1</sup> The certificate may include a QR code.

[*4 March 2025*]

7. The sample of the councillor's insignia is provided in Annex 2 to this Regulation.

8. The councillor's insignia shall contain:

8.1. the name of the local government;

8.2. an image of the local government coat of arms registered in the Register of Coats of Arms or, in the case provided for in the Law On the State Coat of Arms of Latvia, an image of the lesser State coat of arms of the Republic of Latvia;

8.3. the title of the councillor's status - "COUNCILLOR".

[*15 June 2021; 4 March 2025*]

9. Cabinet Regulation No 116 of 14 June 1994, On the Certificate of the Councillor of the Local Government Council, District Council and Parish Council (*Latvijas Vēstnesis*, 1994, No. 73), is repealed.

10. Certificates issued after the 2009 local government elections in accordance with the Cabinet Regulation No 116 of 14 June 1994, On the Certificate of the Councillor of the Local Government Council, District Council and Parish Council, shall be valid until the end of the term of the councillor's mandate.

Prime Minister V. Dombrovskis

Minister for Regional Development and Local Government E. Zalāns
