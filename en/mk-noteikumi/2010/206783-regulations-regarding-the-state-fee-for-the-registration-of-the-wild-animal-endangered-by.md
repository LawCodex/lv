# Regulations Regarding the State Fee for the Registration of the Wild Animal Endangered by the International Trade

Text consolidated by Valsts valodas centrs (State Language Centre) with amending regulations of:

14 December 2010 [shall come into force on 18 December 2010];
17 September 2013 [shall come into force on 1 January 2014].

If a whole or part of a paragraph has been amended, the date of the amending regulation appears in square brackets at the end of the paragraph. If a whole paragraph or sub-paragraph has been deleted, the date of the deletion appears in square brackets beside the deleted paragraph or sub-paragraph.

Republic of Latvia

**Cabinet
Regulation No. 260**
Adopted 16 March 2010

Issued pursuant to
Section 4, Clause 13 of
the Law On the Conservation of Species and Biotopes

1. This Regulation prescribes the amount of the State fee for the registration of the wild animal endangered by the international trade (hereinafter - the endangered animal), as well as the procedures for payment and reliefs of the State fee.

2. The amount of the State fee for the registration of the endangered animal shall be EUR 9.96.

[17 September 2013]

3. The State fee shall be paid prior to submitting an application regarding registration of the endangered animal, paying the respective sum in a credit institution or another institution which has the right to provide payment services.

4. [14 December 2010]

5. The State fee shall be transferred into the State budget.

6. The State fee shall not be paid by State or local government institutions and State or local government capital companies.

[14 December 2010]

7. This Regulation shall come into force on 1 April 2010.

Prime Minister V. Dombrovskis

Minister for Environment R. Vējonis
