# Procedures by which the Provider of Disinfection, Disinsectisation, and Deratisation Services shall Inform about the Commencement of Commercial Activity

25 January 2011 [shall come into force from 28 January 2011].

Republic of Latvia

Cabinet
Regulation No. 350
Adopted 13 April 2010

Issued pursuant to
Section 9, Paragraph eight
of the Chemical Substances Law

1. This Regulation prescribes the procedures by which the merchant shall inform the Health Inspectorate on commencement of the commercial activity - provision of disinfection, disinsectisation, and deratisation services.

2. Prior to commencing the commercial activity - provision of disinfection, disinsectisation, and deratisation services (hereinafter - the service) - the merchant shall submit a submission (Annex) to the Health Inspectorate in person, electronically (if the document has been drawn up in accordance with the laws and regulations regarding drawing up and circulation of electronic documents) or send by post. The submission shall be accompanied by the list of those employees (specialists) who will provide the service and the copies of documents attesting to their education in accordance with the requirements laid down in laws and regulations regarding disinfection, disinsectisation, and deratisation and regarding the required level of education for persons who perform entrepreneurial activity with chemical substances and chemical products.

[25 January 2011]

3. After examination of the submission and documents appended thereto the Health Inspectorate shall inform the merchant if the submission has not been filled in according to the sample provided in the Annex to this Regulation, is incomplete, or documents attesting to the education of the personnel referred to in Paragraph 2 of this Regulation have not been appended. After rectification of deficiencies indicated by the Health Inspectorate the merchant may re-submit the submission. If within one month after submitting the submission the merchant has not received objections of the Health Inspectorate that the submission has not been filled in according to the procedures laid down in this Regulation, it shall be deemed that the merchant has fulfilled the obligation stipulated in Section 9, Paragraph eight of the Chemical Substances Law.

4. If the information indicated in the submission changes, the merchant shall, within 14 days after the relevant changes, submit the information related to changes to the Health Inspectorate.

5. The Health Inspectorate shall publish on its website the list of those merchants who have commenced the provision of the service, and shall update the relevant information at least four times a year.

6. The licences issued to the merchants until the day of coming into force of this Regulation shall be valid until the end of the term of validity indicated in the licence. The merchants who wish to continue the provision of the service after the end of the term of validity indicated in the licence shall submit to the Health Inspectorate the submission referred to in Paragraph 2 of this Regulation not later than one month before the end of the term of validity indicated in the licence.
