# Regulations Regarding Limits for Hotel (Lodging) Expenses for Family Members of an Active Service Soldier, if the Solder Undergoes Medical Treatment outside the Borders of Latvia

Republic of Latvia

**Cabinet
Regulation No. 581**
Adopted 29 June 2010

Issued pursuant to
Section 21, Paragraph six of the Law On Remuneration of
Officials and Employees of State and Local Government Institutions

1. This Regulation prescribes the limits for hotel (lodging) expenses, which are covered by the Ministry of Defence for family members (spouse, children, parents) of an active service soldier, if the solder, in carrying out service duties, has suffered an injury (mutilation, concussion) or such disease, the cause of which is related to performance of military service (occupational disease), and is undergoing medical treatment outside the border of Latvia.

2. The Minister for Defence shall cover hotel (lodging) expenses according to documents confirming expenses and without exceeding the limits laid down in Annex to this Regulation for not more than two family members of the soldier.

3. The Ministry of Defence shall cover hotel (lodging) expenses for not more than seven days during medical treatment of the soldier.

Prime Minister V. Dombrovskis

Acting for the Minister for Defence
who is acting for the Minister of Justice -
Minister for the Interior L. Mūrniece
