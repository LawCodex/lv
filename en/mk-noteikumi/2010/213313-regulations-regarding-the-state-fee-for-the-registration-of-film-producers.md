# Regulations Regarding the State Fee for the Registration of Film Producers

Text consolidated by Valsts valodas centrs (State Language Centre) with amending regulations of:

20 August 2013 [shall come into force from 1 January 2014];
18 December 2018 [shall come into force from 1 January 2019].

If a whole or part of a paragraph has been amended, the date of the amending regulation appears in square brackets at the end of the paragraph. If a whole paragraph or sub-paragraph has been deleted, the date of the deletion appears in square brackets beside the deleted paragraph or sub-paragraph.

Republic of Latvia

**Cabinet
Regulation No. 585**
Adopted 29 June 2010

Issued pursuant to
Section 7, Paragraph two of the Film Law

1. This Regulation prescribes the rate of the State fee for the registration of a film producer and the procedures for its payment.

2. The rate of the State fee for the registration of a film producer is EUR 28.46.

[20 August 2013]

3. The State fee for the registration of a film producer shall be paid before the submission of the application for the registration of the film producer in the National Film Centre through the mediation of such payment service provider who has the right to provide payment services within the meaning of the Law on Payment Services and Electronic Money.

[18 December 2018]

4. The State fee for the registration of a film producer shall be transferred to the revenue account of the State basic budget at the Treasury.

[18 December 2018]

5. Cabinet Regulation No. 486 of 20 November 2001, Regulations Regarding the State Fee for the Registration of Film Producers and Distributors, Film Distribution Points and Films (*Latvijas Vēstnesis*, 2001, No. 171; 2005, No. 172; 2009, No. 206), is repealed.

Prime Minister V. Dombrovskis

Minister for Culture I. Dālderis
