# Regulations Regarding the Amount and Procedures for Payment of the State Fee for the Issuance of a Permit for the Use of Water Resources

Republic of Latvia

**Cabinet
Regulation No. 469**
Adopted 21 June 2011

Issued pursuant to
Section 21, Paragraph 2.<sup>2</sup> of the Water Management Law

1. This Regulation prescribes the amount and procedures for payment of the State fee for the issuance of a permit for the use of water resources (hereinafter - permit).

2. The amount of the State fee for the issuance of a permit is LVL 55.

3. The State fee shall be paid prior to the receipt of the permit using one of the following methods of payment:

3.1. through a credit institution or another institution which has the right to provide payment services within the meaning of the Law On Payment Services and Electronic Money;

3.2. by payment card to the State Environmental Service.

4. The State fee shall be transferred into the State basic budget.

Prime Minister V. Dombrovskis

Acting for the Minister for Environmental Protection
and Regional Development
