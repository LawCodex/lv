# Procedures for the Gathering and Compiling Information Regarding the Prices and Trading Volumes of Agricultural Products in a Specific Reporting Period

Republic of Latvia

**Cabinet
Regulation No. 841**
Adopted 1 November 2011

Issued pursuant to
Section 13, Paragraph one of
the Law On Agriculture and Rural Development

## I. General Provisions

1. This Regulation prescribes the procedures for the gathering and compiling information regarding the prices and trading volumes of agricultural products in a specific reporting period.

2. Information regarding the prices and trading volumes of agricultural products shall be gathered and compiled by the Latvian State Institute of Agrarian Economics (hereinafter - Institute), the Rural Support Service (hereinafter - Service) and State agency "Agricultural Data Centre" (hereinafter - Centre).

3. The Institute, on the basis of data from the Central Statistical Bureau and the Food and Veterinary Service and taking into account the specialisation, economic size and trading volume of production, as well as in order to ensure the representation of information, shall include the following in the group of information providers:

3.1. processors and traders of grain;

3.2. importers and wholesale traders of bananas;

3.3. producers of dairy products;

3.4. producers of hen eggs;

3.5. producers of poultry meat;

3.6. slaughterhouses;

3.7. pig breeders;

3.8. processors and traders of fibre flax and hemp;

3.9. producers of vegetables, fruits and berries.

4. The Service, on the basis of data from the Central Statistical Bureau and taking into account the economic size and specialisation of production, as well as in order to ensure the representation of information, shall include the following in the group of information providers:

4.1. processors of grain;

4.2. processors of potatoes;

4.3. producers of meat products;

4.4. producers of hen eggs.

5. Information regarding the prices and trading volume of imported fishery products shall be provided to the Institute by the Customs Board of the State Revenue Service on the basis of data included in customs declarations.

## II. Content of Information and Procedures for Provision Thereof

6. Each Tuesday by 17.00 the information indicated in Annexes 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 to this Regulation regarding the previous week shall be provided to the Institute by:

6.1. processors and traders of grain (Annex 1);

6.2. importers and wholesale traders of bananas (Annex 2);

6.3. producers of dairy products (Annexes 3 and 4);

6.4. pig breeders (Annex 5);

6.5. producers of hen eggs (Annex 6);

6.6. producers of poultry meat (Annex 7);

6.7. producers of vegetables, fruits and berries (Annex 8).

7. The information indicated in Annex 9 to this Regulation regarding the previous month shall be provided to the Institute by processors and traders of fibre flax and hemp each month by the seventh date.

8. Each year by 1 August the information indicated in Annexes 10 and 11 to this Regulation regarding the previous market year (1 July - 30 June) shall be provided to the Service by:

8.1. processors of grain (Annex 10);

8.2. processors of potatoes (Annex 11).

9. Each year by 1 February the information indicated in Annexes 12 and 13 to this Regulation regarding the previous calendar year shall be provided to the Service by:

9.1. producers of meat products (Annex 12);

9.2. producers of hen eggs (Annex 13).

10. The Customs Board of the State Revenue Service shall provide information regarding the prices and trading volumes of imported fishery products to the Institute within the following time periods:

10.1. regarding the time period from the first to fifteenth date of each month - up to the twenty second date of the same month or up to the first working day after the twenty second date;

10.2. regarding the time period from the sixteenth date of each month up to the end of the month - up to the seventh date of the next month or the first working day after the seventh date.

11. Slaughterhouses, which do not classify animal carcasses in accordance with the regulatory enactments regarding the treatment quality, classification and labelling of animal carcasses, as well as with the procedures by which the conformity of animal carcasses with the requirements for the treatment quality and classification is evaluated and information regarding prices of animal carcasses is gathered and compiled, shall, within 48 hours after slaughtering the animal, in addition to the information specified in the regulatory enactments regarding the procedures for registration of animals, livestock and holdings, as well as marking of animals shall provide the following data to the Centre regarding each bovine animal or pig slaughtered:

11.1. live weight, if such has been specified, and carcass weight;

11.2. value in lats (without value added tax).

12. The Centre shall provide the information referred to in Paragraph 11 of this Regulation regarding the previous week to the Institute each Wednesday by 17.00.

13. The Institute and the Service shall maintain a list of information providers and shall evaluate the group of information providers at least once a year.

14. The Institute and the Service shall inform the Central Statistical Bureau regarding such information providers who have not submitted the necessary information or have submitted incomplete information.

15. The Central Statistical Bureau shall, in accordance with the procedures specified in regulatory enactments, hold the information providers referred to in Paragraph 14 of this Regulation to administrative liability.

## III. Compiling Information

16. A month prior to commencing the compiling of information, the Institute and the Service shall inform an information provider in writing regarding the duty to provide information regarding specific agricultural products in accordance with this Regulation.

17. Information shall be provided by completing a questionnaire in writing in accordance with the relevant Annex to this Regulation and by sending such questionnaire or by telephone within the time period specified in this Regulation, except the information referred to in Paragraph 11 of this Regulation. The information provider shall store signed questionnaires (originals) for at least a year regardless of the way of providing the information (except the case referred to in Paragraph 11 of this Regulation).

18. Information provided by information providers shall be considered restricted access information, and publishing thereof shall be admissible only in the form of summary in accordance with the Freedom of Information Law.

19. The Institute shall compile and send the information gathered in accordance with the procedures specified in this Regulation, using electronic means, to the European Commission and the Ministry of Agriculture.

20. The Service shall compile and send the information gathered in accordance with the procedures specified in this Regulation, using electronic means, to the Ministry of Agriculture, the Central Statistical Bureau and the European Statistical Bureau EUROSTAT.

## IV. Control of the Conformity of Information

21. The Institute or an authorised person thereof (hereinafter - controller) shall perform control of the conformity of information in undertakings of the information providers referred to in Paragraphs 6 and 7 of this Regulation. The Food and Veterinary Service shall perform control of the conformity of information in undertakings of the information providers referred to in Paragraph 11 of this Regulation.

22. The controller and the Food and Veterinary Service has the right to visit an information provider without prior warning and to request all necessary information and documentation confirming the conformity of prices and trading volumes.

23. An information provider has a duty to provide the information requested by the controller and the Food and Veterinary Service regarding registration, compiling and notification of prices and trading volume.

## V. Closing Provision

24. Cabinet Regulation No. 367 of 20 April 2004, *Procedures by which Information regarding the Prices and Trading Volumes of Agricultural Products shall be Gathered and Compiled* (*Latvijas Vēstnesis*, 2004, No. 69, 210; 2006, No. 172; 2008, No. 179), is repealed.
