Republic of Latvia

**Cabinet
Regulation No. 761**
Adopted 3 September 2013

Issued pursuant to
Section 3, Paragraph six, Section 7, Paragraph three,
and Section 19, Paragraph five
of the Law on Registration of Civil Status Acts

## I. General Provisions

1. The Regulation prescribes the following for civil status acts (conclusion of marriage, fact of birth or death):

1.1. registration procedures;

1.2. sample entries of registers;

1.3. information to be entered in an entry of a register;

1.4. procedures for the storage, updating (correcting, supplementing or cancelling) of register entries and their renewal;

1.5. procedures for issuing information and its extent;

1.6. samples of documents that shall be submitted for the registration of civil status acts;

1.7. samples of documents that shall be issued based on register entries.

2. A civil status act shall be registered by a local government General Registry Office (hereinafter - the General Registry Office) or a diplomatic and consular mission of the Republic of Latvia in a foreign country (hereinafter - the mission) pursuant to the exceptions determined in the Law on Registration of Civil Status Acts.

3. Conclusion of marriage, registration of the fact of birth or death may not be done in the same premises at the same time.

4. A civil status act shall be registered, updated or renewed and an entry in a register of civil status acts (hereinafter - the register entry) shall be entered in the Register of Natural Persons. The register entry which has been drawn up before 31 December 2012 shall be considered a historical register entry.

[*11 May 2021 / The new wording of the Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

5. The General Registry Office, the mission and the Ministry of Justice shall, before entry of information in the Register of Natural Persons and before repeated issuing of a document confirming the registration of a civil status act, verify the provided information with the Register of Natural Persons.

[*11 May 2021 / The new wording of the Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

6. Entry of information on the registration, updating, and renewal of the register entry and historical register entry shall be commenced by entering the personal identity number of the person. If information on a person has not been entered in the Register of Natural Persons and the person has not been identified, information shall be entered according to the presented documents or the historical register entry, and an automatically generated individual personal identity number shall not be assigned.

[*11 May 2021 / The new wording of the Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

7. A person's given name (names) and surname shall be entered in the register entry in the official language in accordance with the legal acts regarding the spelling and use of personal names in Latvian, their reproduction (also by transliterating the original foreign language form in Roman alphabet), alignment and identification.

8. If information on a foreigner needs to be entered in the register entry, personal names shall be reproduced in accordance with Paragraph 7 of this Regulation. Should a person have such wish, the given name and surname of a foreigner may be entered in the register entry according to a personal identification document of the foreigner by transliterating the original foreign language form in Roman alphabet.

9. When entering information in the Register of Natural Persons on the historical register entry, the given name and surname of a person of legal age shall be indicated according to the historical register entry but the personal identity number, ethnicity, nationality and its type shall be entered in accordance with the current information in the Register of Natural Persons.

[*11 May 2021 / The new wording of the Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

10. If the responsible person of the General Registry Office is not able to communicate orally or in writing with a person with a disability or with a person without literacy skills, information on the person with a disability or the person without literacy skills shall be entered in the register entry in the presence of such aid person who is able to communicate with the abovementioned person and who certifies by his or her signature that the information entered in the register entry corresponds to the intent of the person with a disability or the intent of the person without literacy skills. If the person is able to express his or her intent orally and is able to read the information entered in the register entry, the presence of an aid person is not mandatory. If the person is not able to write but can read, the person shall read the information entered in the register entry and confirm that such information corresponds to the intent of the person. If the person is able to read the information entered in the register entry and is able to write, he or she shall write by hand that he or she has read the information entered in the register entry and that it correctly reflects the intent of such person. The responsible person of the General Registry Office shall, to the extent possible, use types and means of alternative communication to communicate with the abovementioned person. A note thereof shall be made in the section of notes of the register entry. Such procedures shall be applied also when the person with a disability or the person without literacy skills is the declarant of a civil status act.

[*11 May 2021*]

11. Information shall be entered in the Register of Natural Persons based on historical register entry. If supplementations or corrections have been made to the historical register entry until 31 December 2012, information shall be entered in the Register of Natural Persons in accordance with the supplementations or corrections made.

[*21 June 2016; 11 May 2021; Amendment to Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

12. When entering information in the Register of Natural Persons on a historical register entry and issuing a certificate or statement from a register of civil status acts, the registration institution shall be indicated in accordance with the historical register entry. Place names (the place of birth of a newborn, the place of birth of spouses, and the place of birth and death of a deceased person) shall be entered in accordance with the administrative territorial division at the moment of repeated issuing of a document. If this is not possible due to changes in administrative territorial division, the place names shall be indicated in accordance with the territorial division that existed on the date of drawing up the register entry.

[*11 May 2021; Amendment to Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

13. Should the General Registry Office or mission consider that additional information needs to be entered in the register entry, it shall be done in the section for notes.

14. The information entered in the register entry shall be read out to the declarant of a civil status act.

15. Information entered in the Register of Natural Persons may be corrected without drawing up an opinion only at the date of its entry. The register entry may be later corrected in accordance with the procedures laid down in Chapter IX of this Regulation.

[*11 May 2021; Amendment to Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

16. Each register entry shall have a separate numbering within a calendar year.

17. A computer printout of an entry in a register of civil status acts (hereinafter - the printout), a certificate of the registration of civil status acts that conforms to the specifications referred to in Annex 1 to this Regulation, or a statement on the registration of civil status acts shall be printed out after entry of information in the Register of Natural Persons. The abovementioned documents (except when the issue of a document confirming the registration of civil status acts is mandatory) shall be issued to the persons indicated in the Law on Registration of Civil Status Acts upon request. A statement on the registration of civil status acts may be issued with a secure electronic signature.

[*11 May 2021; Amendment to Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

18. The scope of information which shall be issued from the Register of Natural Persons shall be determined in the printout and certificate. If a person wishes to obtain additional information other than specified in the printout and certificate, a statement shall be issued from the Register of Natural Persons.

[*11 May 2021; Amendment to Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

19. The Ministry of Justice shall issue marriage certificate forms to the persons authorised by a denomination management by issuing a warrant (Annex 2). The authorised person shall verify the number, series and numbers of marriage certificate forms at the moment of their receipt. The denomination management shall hand over the received blank marriage certificate forms to ministers.

20. A denomination management shall be responsible for the registration, storage and use of marriage certificate forms.

21. If marriage certificate forms are lost, a denomination management shall immediately inform the State Police and the Ministry of Justice thereof, indicating the number, series and numbers of forms and the circumstances under which they were lost.

22. When examining a case for the updating, correction or supplementation of the register entry, the General Registry Office shall, upon complete clarification of all the circumstances, provide its opinion in accordance with the procedures laid down in the Administrative Procedure Law. If the opinion is positive, all the information that shall be updated, corrected or supplemented shall be indicated in this opinion. If the opinion is negative, the reason for refusal and appeal procedures shall be indicated in the opinion.

[*21 June 2016*]

23. The nomenclature of the General Registry Office shall be developed according to the sample nomenclature developed by the Ministry of Justice.

24. The General Registry Office shall, each year by 10 January and 10 July, and the mission shall, by 15 January and 15 July, submit to the Ministry of Justice a report on the civil status acts registered in a half-year period (Annex 3).

25. A denomination management shall, each year by 10 January and 10 July, submit to the Ministry of Justice a report on the use of marriage certificate forms.

## II. Registration of Marriage

26. In order to conclude marriage at the General Registry Office, at the mission or by a minister, persons who wish to conclude marriage shall personally submit a joint submission of a specific sample (Annex 4) and the documents required for registering the marriage to the General Registry Office or the mission for the verification of documents, except in the case referred to in Paragraph 26.<sup>1</sup> of this Regulation.

[*21 August 2018*]

26.<sup>1</sup> Latvian citizens of legal age or Latvian non-citizens of legal age may submit a submission for the conclusion of marriage (Annex 4) also electronically by sending it to the e-mail address of the General Registry Office in which they wish to conclude marriage if the submission is signed with a secure electronic signature and it has a time stamp (hereinafter - the electronic submission).

[*21 August 2018*]

27. The mission shall accept submissions only from Latvian citizens and Latvian non-citizens residing in a foreign country.

28. The responsible person of the General Registry Office or the mission shall acquaint the persons with the norms of the Civil Law that establish the procedures for concluding marriage and with the impediments to the conclusion of marriage.

29. The responsible person of the General Registry Office or the mission shall compare the information indicated in the submission with the documents presented and submitted by the persons.

29.<sup>1</sup> The responsible person of the General Registry Office shall check the electronic submission within five working days following its receipt. If the columns indicated in the electronic submission have not been filled in or have been filled in incorrectly or the electronic submission has not been signed by both persons who wish to conclude marriage or if the State fee for the registration of marriage has not been paid, the responsible person of the General Registry Office shall electronically notify thereof the persons who wish to conclude marriage and specify a period of five working days for the rectification of the established shortcomings.

[*21 August 2018*]

29.<sup>2</sup> If the shortcomings referred to in Paragraph 29.<sup>1</sup> of this Regulation have not been rectified within the specified period, the electronic submission shall be deemed as not submitted and the responsible person of the General Registry Office shall electronically notify thereof the persons who wish to conclude marriage.

[*21 August 2018*]

29.<sup>3</sup> If the columns indicated in the electronic submission have been completed, the electronic submission has been signed by both persons who wish to conclude marriage and the State fee for the registration of marriage has been paid or if the shortcomings have been rectified within the specified period, the responsible person of the General Registry Office shall compare the information indicated in the electronic submission with the information entered in the Register of Natural Persons. If the abovementioned information is consistent, the responsible person of the General Registry Office shall send a statement on the acceptance of the electronic submission to the persons who wish to conclude marriage.

[*21 August 2018; 11 May 2021; Amendment to Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

29.<sup>4</sup> If the information indicated in the electronic submission is not consistent with the information entered in the Register of Natural Persons, the responsible person of the General Registry Office shall electronically notify thereof the persons who wish to conclude marriage and specify a period of five working days for the rectification of non-conformities. If the non-conformities are not rectified within the specified period, the electronic submission shall be deemed as not submitted and the responsible person of the General Registry Office shall electronically notify thereof the persons who wish to conclude marriage.

[*21 August 2018; 11 May 2021; Amendment to Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

30. If documents issued by foreign authorities that are required for the conclusion of marriage are submitted to the General Registry Office or mission, the institution which issued the document and also the date of issue shall be indicated in the register entry.

[*23 April 2024*]

31. Declaration on the marital status for the conclusion of marriage in the Republic of Latvia (Annex 5) of a person who has been granted the status of a stateless person, a refugee or an alternative status in the Republic of Latvia, and also information on a document that grants the person the right to lawfully reside in the Republic of Latvia, and also the term of validity thereof, shall be indicated on the section for notes of the register entry.

32. If, besides the citizenship of the Republic of Latvia, a person also has a citizenship of another country, a document on marital status issued by the competent authority of the other country shall also be submitted for the conclusion of marriage in the Republic of Latvia. A foreigner who has several nationalities or has established legal links with several countries in the field of employment law or education shall submit documents issued by the competent authorities of all the countries concerned regarding the marital status. In the notes section of the register entry, information on the nationality of the person and its type shall be indicated.

[*21 June 2016;31 March 2020*]

33. The document on marital status issued by a foreign competent authority for the conclusion of marriage in Latvia shall be valid for six months after the day of its issuing, unless a shorter term of validity has been indicated in the document.

34. If a person wishes to conclude marriage with a person with disabilities, a request for submitting the submission outside the premises of the General Registry Office shall be submitted to the General Registry Office according to the place of location of the person with disabilities. The request shall indicate information on the persons wishing to conclude marriage (given name, surname, personal identity number) and shall be accompanied by an opinion issued by a medical treatment institution stating that the person with disabilities is not transportable.

35. The persons shall agree with the responsible person of the General Registry Office or the mission on the time (day and hour) and place for the registration of marriage.

36. If necessary, the time and place for the conclusion of marriage may be changed.

37. If persons wish to conclude marriage in another General Registry Office, mission or by a minister, the General Registry Office or the mission in which the submission for the conclusion of marriage was submitted shall issue a statement on the verification of the documents necessary for the conclusion of marriage (Annex 6). The submission shall be retained in the General Registry Office or the mission where it was submitted, but the statement - in the General Registry Office, the mission or by the minister where marriage was concluded.

38. If the marriage is not concluded within six months after receipt of the submission and the persons still wish to conclude marriage, the submission shall be submitted repeatedly.

39. Marriage shall be registered in the mission, the General Registry Office or another place suitable for the conclusion of marriage located within the administrative territory of a local government or by a minister.

40. A person with disabilities who, based on an opinion of a medical treatment institution, is not transportable shall register marriage in the presence of a medical practitioner in accordance with the place of location of the person with disabilities.

41. If marriage is registered outside the premises of the General Registry Office, the place for the conclusion of marriage shall be indicated in the section for notes of the register entry. When registering marriage in the case referred to in Paragraph 34 of this Regulation, information on the submitter of the request referred to in Paragraph 34 of this Regulation (given name, surname, personal identity number) and date of submission of the request shall be indicated in the section for notes of the register entry.

42. When concluding marriage with a foreigner and obtaining the surname of the spouse, the original form of the chosen surname may also be indicated in the register entry in transliteration in the Roman alphabet, if a person so wishes. The original form of the surname in transliteration in the Roman alphabet shall be entered in the register entry according to a personal identification document of the foreigner (the other spouse).

[*21 June 2016*]

43. After entry of the information in the Register of Natural Persons, the printout shall be printed (Annex 7). It shall be signed by the persons referred to in the Law on Registration of Civil Status Acts.

[*11 May 2021; Amendment to Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

44. After concluding a marriage, a marriage certificate shall be issued:

44.1. by the General Registry Office or the mission (Annex 8);

44.2. by a minister (Annex 9).

45. If the entry of information in the Register of Natural Persons cannot be technically ensured at the place where the marriage is concluded, a protocol on the conclusion of marriage (Annex 10) shall be completed. The protocol shall be signed by both spouses, the witness(es), if the witness(es) participate in the registration of marriage, and the official of the General Registry Office. The protocol shall be approved with a seal with the State coat of arms.

[*23 April 2024*]

46. The General Registry Office which registered the marriage shall keep the protocol together with the printout of the entry in the Marriage Register and bind it into the book in accordance with the procedures referred to in Chapter VI of this Regulation.

[*21 June 2016*]

47. Information on the concluded marriage shall be entered in the Register of Natural Persons not later than on the next working day after drawing up of the protocol. After making an entry in the Marriage Register, a marriage certificate shall be issued to the spouses or sent by post if such request has been made.

[*11 May 2021; Amendment to Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

48. Upon receiving a notice of a minister on a concluded marriage, the General Registry Office shall enter the relevant information in the Register of Natural Persons. The given name, surname of the minister, and the date of receipt of the notice shall be indicated in the section for notes of the register entry. If the notice of the minister is inaccurate or erroneous, the information shall not be entered in the Register of Natural Persons, but the notice of the minister shall be immediately sent back to be corrected or supplemented.

[*11 May 2021; Amendment to Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

48.<sup>1</sup> If the marriage has been concluded by a minister, the church congregation in which the marriage has been registered and the entry of the Marriage Register drawn up by the minister is stored shall be indicated in the entry of the Marriage Register as the place of the conclusion of marriage.

[*21 August 2018*]

## III. Registration of Marriage at Prison

49. A marriage can be registered at a prison by an official of the General Registry Office in the territory of which the prison is located or by a minster. An official of other General Registry Office may register marriage if:

49.1. the person has entered the prison after the submission was submitted to the General Registry Office;

49.2. the prisoner has been transferred to another prison;

49.3. prisoners wish that marriage was registered by an official of the General Registry Office in the operational territory of which the prison of the other prisoner is located.

50. A submission for the conclusion of marriage (Annex 11) and the documents required for the conclusion of marriage (except for the documents held by the prison administration) shall be presented by the person who wishes to conclude marriage with a prisoner. If the person does not have the necessary documents for the conclusion of marriage or if both persons are in prison, the documents shall be requested from State and local government authorities with the intermediation of an authorised person or the prison administration. The submission shall be submitted to the General Registry Office in the operational territory of which the prison is located.

51. The General Registry Office shall issue to the person a statement on the verification of the documents required for the conclusion of marriage (Annex 12) for submission to a minister or another General Registry Office in the cases referred to in Sub-paragraphs 49.1, 49.2, and 49.3 of this Regulation.

52. The prison administration shall ensure the safety of the officials of the General Registry Office and participants to the conclusion of marriage, and also the conclusion of marriage at the set time and in suitable premises of the prison.

53. When a marriage is concluded at a prison, an entry on the conclusion of marriage at a prison shall be made in the protocol (Annex 13). The protocol shall be signed by both spouses, the witness(es), if the witness(es) participate in the registration of marriage, and the official of the General Registry Office. The protocol shall be approved with a seal with the State coat of arms.

[*23 April 2024*]

54. Based on the protocol on the conclusion of marriage at a prison, the official of the General Registry Office shall enter information on the conclusion of marriage in the Register of Natural Persons no later than on the next working day after the conclusion of marriage. A marriage certificate shall be issued in person or at request of the spouses - sent by post to an indicated address. The General Registry Office shall send a copy of the marriage certificate to the respective prison for inclusion in the prisoner's file.

[*11 May 2021; Amendment to Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

54.<sup>1</sup> The General Registry Office which has registered marriage in a prison shall store the protocol together with the printout of the entry in the Marriage Register and bind it into the book in accordance with the procedures referred to in Chapter VI of this Regulation.

[*21 June 2016*]

55. All expenditures related to the conclusion of marriage shall be covered by the persons wishing to conclude marriage.

56. A person who wishes to conclude marriage with a prisoner shall submit to the General Registry Office a completed submission, indicating all the necessary information, with the exception of the information on the other person who is at a prison, and present the documents required for the conclusion of marriage.

57. An official of the General Registry Office shall compare the information indicated in the submission with the presented documents, certify it with his or her signature and a seal with the State coat of arms, and issue it to the person who wishes to conclude marriage with a prisoner.

58. The person who wishes to conclude marriage with a prisoner shall send the submission to the respective prison administration by post or submit in person. It shall be indicated in the submission whether the person wishes to receive the completed submission by post or in person.

59. The prison administration shall hand over the received submission to the prisoner. The abovementioned person shall complete the submission, indicating information on himself or herself, and append the documents required for the conclusion of marriage.

60. The head of the prison shall certify the signature of the prisoner with a signature and seal. The submission and the documents required for the conclusion of marriage shall be issued in person or sent by post to the person who wishes to conclude marriage with a prisoner.

61. An official of the General Registry Office shall, when accepting a submission or a statement on the verification of the documents required for the conclusion of marriage, or a minister shall, when accepting a statement on the verification of the documents required for the conclusion of marriage, agree with the prison administration and the person who wishes to conclude marriage with a prisoner on the day and time for the conclusion of marriage.

62. If both persons who wish to conclude marriage are in prison, they shall inform the prison administration in writing about their wish to conclude marriage.

63. The prisons administrations shall agree in which prison the marriage will be concluded.

64. A representative of the prison administration where the marriage will be concluded shall provide the prisoner with the submission form.

65. The prisoner shall indicate information on himself or herself in the submission form, and append the documents required for the conclusion of marriage.

66. The head of the prison shall certify the signature of the prisoner with a signature and seal, and send the submission together with the documents required for the conclusion of marriage to the prison administration of the other prisoner.

67. The other prisoner shall indicate information on himself or herself in the submission form, and append the documents required for the conclusion of marriage. The head of the prison shall certify the signature of the prisoner with a signature and seal, and send the submission together with the documents required for the conclusion of marriage to the General Registry Office in the territory of which the marriage will be concluded.

68. If marriage is concluded by a minister or officials of another General Registry Office, the prison administration shall, upon request of the person, require the General Registry office to issue a statement on the verification of the documents required for the conclusion of marriage for sending it to a minister or the other General Registry Office.

69. An official of the General Registry Office shall, when accepting a submission or statement on the verification of the documents required for the conclusion of marriage, or a minister shall, when accepting a statement on the verification of the documents required for the conclusion of marriage, agree with the prison administration on the day and time for the conclusion of marriage of the prisoners.

## IV. Registration of the Fact of Birth

70. When notifying of the birth of a child, a medical certificate issued by a medical treatment institution or a medical practitioner that confirms the fact of birth, sex of the child, place and time of the birth, given name and surname of the mother (hereinafter - the medical certificate that confirms the fact of birth), or another document issued by a medical treatment institution or a medical practitioner that confirms the fact that a woman has given birth and contains information on the sex of the child and the probable time of the birth shall be submitted to the General Registry Office or the mission.

71. If the fact of the birth of a child is notified more than a month after the birth of the child, the General Registry Office or the mission shall register it based on the following documents:

71.1. a submission by the father or mother or both parents, or other interested persons notifying of the birth of a child, ascertaining the circumstances of delay;

71.2. a medical certificate certifying the fact of birth, or a document issued by a medical treatment institution or a medical practitioner on the sex of the child and the probable time of birth.

72. The responsible official of the General Registry Office or the mission shall register the fact of birth on the day of receipt of the notification on the birth of a child in the presence of the declarant. If a written notification on the fact of the birth of a child has been received in the cases specified in the Law on Registration of Civil Status Acts, the responsible official of the General Registry Office or the mission shall register the fact of birth on the day of receipt of the written notification. The date and form of receipt of the written notification shall be indicated in the section for notes of the register entry.

73. Upon a wish of the parents, in addition to the surname of the child, the original form of the chosen surname in transliteration in the Roman alphabet may be indicated in the register entry. The original form of the surname in transliteration in the Roman alphabet shall be entered according to a personal identification document of a foreigner (father or mother).

74. When registering the fact of the birth of a child, an automatically generated individual personal identity number shall be assigned to the child in the Register of Natural Persons and the nationality of the child and its type shall not be indicated if:

74.1. the child is three months old;

74.2. both parents are foreigners at the moment when the child is born;

74.3. one of the parents is a non-citizen of the Republic of Latvia or a stateless person and the other is a citizen of another country at the moment when the child is born;

74.4. the child has been born abroad and his or her parents are non-citizens of the Republic of Latvia or stateless persons at the moment when the child is born.

[*11 May 2021 / The new wording of the Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

75. The address of the place of residence of a child shall be indicated in the entry in the Birth Register in compliance with the following conditions:

75.1. upon the instructions of the father or mother of the child or his or her authorised person, declare the place of residence of the newborn child, concurrently indicating the information for the declaration of a place of residence specified in the Declaration of Place of Residence Law;

75.2. upon the instructions of the medical practitioner, other persons present at birth, the head of the shelter or the prison or local government, or in the case where there is no basis for declaring the place of residence, the address in the entry in the Birth Register shall be registered as that indicated;

75.3. the address of the place of residence of a stillborn child or a child who died at birth shall not be declared and shall not be indicated;

75.4. the address shall be declared and indicated in accordance with the address included in the State Address Register.

[*21 June 2016*]

76. The nationality of the child shall be indicated in the register entry in accordance with the ethnicity of a relative in direct ascending line within two generations if the parents can prove relationship with the abovementioned person.

77. When recognising paternity, the parents shall submit a joint submission for the recognition of paternity (Annex 14) or a submission addressed to the General Registry Office under which the authenticity of the submitter's signature is certified in the case laid down in Section 35, Paragraph two of the Law on Registration of Civil Status Acts. Paternity shall be recognised also if, upon notifying the fact of the birth of a child, a joint submission (Annex 15) of the mother of the child, the husband of the mother of the child or the former husband of the mother of the child and the biological father of the child is submitted in person to the General Registry Office or the mission, or a submission addressed to the General Registry Office is submitted under which the authenticity of the submitters' signatures is certified.

78. If, when notifying the fact of the birth of a child, paternity is recognised at the same time in the presence of both parents, both parents shall be indicated in the register entry as declarants. Both declarants shall sign in the columns "basis for entering information on the father" and "declarant" of the printouts of the entry in the Birth Register.

79. If the surname of the mother indicated in the medical certificate does not correspond to the surname of the mother indicated in a personal identification document at the moment of the registration of the fact of the birth of a child, the surname of the mother shall be entered in the entry in the Birth Register in accordance with the surname of the mother indicated in the personal identification document.

80. If other mistakes in the personal data of the mother are found in the medical certificate confirming the birth of a child at the moment of the registration of the fact of the birth of a child, information on the mother shall be included in the entry in the Birth Register in accordance with the personal identification document of the mother.

81. If the mother has not presented a personal identification document when being admitted at a medical treatment institution and leaves the child in the medical treatment institution when being discharged from the medical treatment institution after childbirth, the child shall be registered as a foundling.

82. In order to register the fact of the birth of a stillborn child or a child who died at birth, a medical certificate shall be issued by a medical treatment institution or a medical practitioner that confirms perinatal death, indicating the fact of birth, the date of birth, the sex of the child, the place and time of birth, the given name and surname of the mother (hereinafter - the medical certificate of perinatal death).

82.<sup>1</sup> In order to re-compile the entry of the Birth Register in the cases specified in the Law on Registration of Civil Status Acts, the parents of the child shall submit the following to the General Registry Office or the mission:

82.<sup>1</sup>1. the submission with the request to re-register the fact of the birth of the child;

82.<sup>1</sup>2. the document confirming the registration of the fact of birth which has been issued in the country in which the first entry of the fact of birth of the child was registered;

82.<sup>1</sup>3. the document regarding the refusal to make a supplementation to the entry of the birth register of the child issued by the competent authority of a foreign country in which the first entry of the fact of birth of the child was registered.

[*21 August 2018*]

83. After entry of information in the Register of Natural Persons, the printout shall be printed (Annex 16). It shall be signed by the persons referred to in the Law on Registration of Civil Status Acts.

[*11 May 2021; Amendment to Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

84. After registering the fact of the birth of a child, the General Registry Office or the mission shall issue a birth certificate (Annex 17).

## V. Registration of the Fact of Death

85. The General Registry Office or the mission shall register the fact of death on the day of receipt of a notification.

86. When notifying the fact of death, the declarant shall present to the General Registry Office or the mission a personal identification document and submit:

86.1. the personal identification document of the deceased person;

86.2. the medical certificate of perinatal death, a medical certificate issued by a medical treatment institution or a medical practitioner on the cause of death or other document confirming the fact of death.

87. The institutions referred to in the Law on Registration of Civil Status Acts or another legal person shall notify of the fact of death in writing. Information on the institution or legal person that notified of the fact of death shall be entered in the section for notes of the register entry.

[*21 June 2016*]

87.<sup>1</sup> When registering the fact of the death of such identified person who has not been registered in the Register of Natural Persons, an automatically generated individual personal identity number shall not be assigned to the person.

[*11 May 2021 / New wording of the Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

88. If it becomes known when registering the fact of death that minor children of the deceased person are left without parental care, the General Registry Office shall on the same day notify the Orphan's and Custody Court according to the place of residence of the children. If the place of residence of a minor child is not in the Republic of Latvia, the General Registry Office or the mission shall notify the Orphan's and Custody Court according to the last place of residence of the child or parents in the Republic of Latvia.

89. When registering the fact of death, the section for notes of the register entry shall indicate whether a personal identification document of the deceased person has or has not been submitted to the General Registry Office or the mission. When registering the fact of the death of a foreigner, the General Registry Office shall additionally indicate in the section for notes of the register entry the number, issuing authority, and date of issue of the received personal identification document.

90. The personal identification documents of deceased persons registered by the General Registry Office shall be handed over to the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs once per month together with a list. The personal identification documents of deceased persons registered by the mission shall be handed over to the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs with the closest diplomatic mail.

91. Once a month, the General Registry Office shall hand over the official identification documents of foreigners whose fact of death has been registered in the Republic of Latvia to the accredited diplomatic and consular mission of the issuing country of the identification documents of the deceased person in Latvia. If the diplomatic and consular mission of the relevant country is not located in Latvia, the personal identification documents of the deceased foreigner shall be handed over to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs for dispatch to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the relevant country.

[*21 August 2018*]

92. When registering the fact of the death of an unknown (unidentified) person, information in the entry of the Death Register shall be entered in accordance with the information indicated in a medical certificate of perinatal death, in a certificate issued by a medical treatment institution or a medical practitioner on the cause of death or in another document confirming the fact of death. An automatically generated individual personal identity number shall not be assigned to the person in the Register of Natural Persons.

[*11 May 2021; Amendment to Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

93. If the exact time of death is not known, the time of death in the register entry shall be indicated as follows:

93.1. registration date of the death if the death has been registered in the month when the person died;

93.2. registration date of the death if the death was registered in the year when the person died;

93.3. the last day of the month of the death and the year if the death was registered in another month and year;

93.4. the last day of the year of the death if the death was registered in another year;

93.5. the date when the person was taken to the Thanatology Department (morgue) of the State Centre for Forensic Medical Examination of the Republic of Latvia that is indicated in the opinion of the forensic medical expert-examination or another document confirming the fact of death.

94. If the time of death has been indicated in accordance with Paragraph 93 of this Regulation, a note on the approximation of the date shall be made at the date of the death in the entry in the Death Register and the approximate date shall be written in the form of text in the notes.

95. After entry of information in the Register of Natural Persons, the printout shall be printed (Annex 18). It shall be signed by the persons referred to in the Law on Registration of Civil Status Acts.

[*11 May 2021; Amendment to Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

96. After registration of the fact of death, the General Registry Office or the mission shall issue a death certificate (Annex 19) upon request.

97. The General Registry Office and the mission shall, once a month together with an accompanying document, hand over the documents confirming the fact of death to the responsible institution of the Ministry of Health.

## VI. Storage of Register Entries

98. The first copies of the historic register entries shall be stored in the General Registry Office of the republic city or in the General Registry Office which is located in the administrative centre of the county and the second copies - in the General Registry Office of the republic city or in the General Registry Office of the administrative centre of the county or in the Ministry of Justice.

[*31 March 2020*]

99. Riga City General Registry Office shall retain the first copies of register entries which have been drawn up:

99.1. in Riga City General Registry Offices;

99.2. in missions.

100. Register entries of ministers of the denominations indicated in Section 51 of the Civil Law which have been drawn up from 1 September 1993 shall be retained by the respective denomination.

101. The printout shall be retained by the General Registry Office and the mission which registered the civil status act. The General Registry Office which is not located in the administrative centre of a municipality shall, each year by 31 January, hand over the printouts for the previous calendar year, bound in books, to the General Registry Office located in the administrative centre of the municipality.

102. Riga City General Registry Offices and the missions shall, each year by 31 January, hand over the printouts for the previous calendar year, bound in books, to the Riga City General Registry Office.

103. Printouts shall be bound in books according to the type of register arranged in chronological order of register entries. No less than 75 and no more than 250 printouts shall be bound in a book of register entries.

104. If a small number of register entries have been drawn up within a year, printouts of all types of entries made during the year may be combined in one book of register entries.

105. If a large number of one type of register entries have been drawn up in a General Registry Office within a year, printouts shall be divided and bound in several books of register entries.

106. Before binding, the cover page where the place, year, type of the drawing up of printouts, and also the table of content of the book shall be added to a book of register entries.

107. If printouts of one type and year of register entries have been divided for binding into several books of register entries, the first and the last number of the printouts bound in the book, and the date of drawing up of the first and the last printout shall be additionally indicated before binding for each book of register entries.

108. Before binding, the final page where the number of pages to be bound in the book of register entries (in numbers and words) shall be added to the books of register entries, which shall be approved with a signature of the head of the General Registry Office and a seal with the State coat of arms.

109. The bound books of register entries shall be numbered and recorded in the archive collection accounting journal (Annex 20) of the General Registry Office which retains the books of register entries until they are handed over to the National Archives of Latvia.

110. The General Registry Office shall retain the books of register entries in accordance with the technical requirements for the storage of archival records - in archive storage premises. Archival records for which no archive storage has been created shall be retained in locked cabinets or premises that are not accessible to visitors.

111. The head of the General Registry Office shall be responsible for the storage of the archive of the General Registry Office.

## VII. Renewal of Register Entries

112. Renewal of the register entry concerns historical register entries.

113. A registered but destroyed or lost register entry which has been drawn up in the Republic of Latvia on the basis of an opinion shall be renewed by the General Registry Office which has received a submission for the renewal of the register entry.

114. The register entry which has been registered by a mission but has been destroyed or lost shall be renewed by the Riga City General Registry Office.

115. To determine whether the register entry has been destroyed or lost, the respective type of registers shall be verified for a period of three years before and three years after the drawing up of the register entry.

116. The fact that the register entry has been destroyed or lost shall be confirmed by a statement from the General Registry Office where the register entry to be renewed was drawn up, or from the General Registry Office where the first and second copies of the register entry must be stored, or by a statement from the Ministry of Justice if the second copy of the register entry is stored in the Ministry of Justice.

[*23 April 2024*]

117. The statement issued by the General Registry Office where the register entry to be renewed was drawn up or by the General Registry Office where the first and second copies of the register entry must be stored, and the statement issued by the Ministry of Justice shall indicate that the register has not been retained and has not been renewed previously.

[*23 April 2024*]

118. The General Registry Office shall renew an entry in the Marriage Register if both spouses or one of them is alive.

119. An entry in the Birth Register on a minor shall be renewed on the basis of a submission of the child's lawful representative. A minor who has reached 15 years of age shall have the right to submit a submission for the renewal of the register entry if he or she has a personal identification document.

120. The following documents shall be appended to a submission for the renewal of the register entry:

120.1. the statements referred to in Paragraph 116 of this Regulation;

120.2. a list of biographical data of the submitter (person whose entry shall be renewed) required for the renewal of the register entry;

120.3. documents confirming the fact of registration of the destroyed or lost register entry and other documents confirming the information required for renewal of the information indicated by the submitter;

120.4. an explanation if conflicting information has been found in the documents on the person whose register entry is being renewed.

121. To support the submission and clarify the conflicting information, the General Registry Office shall be entitled to make a reasoned request for information from State and local government institutions and authorities.

122. If the documents of the renewal case conflicting or ambiguous information on a person's identity, the General Registry Office shall send the case to the territorial institution of the State Police according to the place of residence of the submitter. The territorial institution of the State Police shall verify the personal data. The opinion on the verification results shall be sent back to the General Registry Office together with the case.

123. The opinion on the renewal of the register entry shall be drawn up in one copy, and together with the case retained by the General Registry Office.

124. An entry shall be renewed immediately after the drawing up of an opinion and the information shall be entered in the Register of Natural Persons. The date when the information has been entered in the Register of Natural Persons shall be considered the date of the compilation of the Register.

[*11 May 2021 / The new wording of the Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

125. When renewing a birth entry, the date of birth of the person and information on the parents shall be determined based on the documents present in the case.

126. When renewing the register entry, the registration institution shall be indicated in accordance with the name of the General Registry Office which renewed the register entry. The name of the registration institution where the register entry to be renewed was drawn up shall be indicated in the section for notes of the register entry.

127. After renewal of the register entry, the printout of the renewed register entry shall be printed out from the Register of Natural Persons. It shall be retained according to a type of register together with other printouts.

[*11 May 2021; Amendment to Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

128. Based on the renewed register entry, a certificate on the registration of a civil status act shall be issued upon request.

129. An opinion to refuse to renew the register entry shall be drawn up in two copies. One copy of the opinion shall be issued to the person who has submitted the submission for the renewal of the register entry, the other shall be retained by the General Registry Office.

## VIII. Supplementations to Register Entries

130. The register entry may be supplemented if it has been drawn up in the General Registry Office or the mission. The register entry shall be supplemented by the General Registry Office which has received the information or submission for the supplementation of the register entry.

131. If the mission has received a submission for the supplementation of the register entry which has been drawn up by the General Registry Office or the mission, the General Registry Office which retains the register entry to be renewed shall process the case for the supplementation of the register entry and supplement the register entry accordingly.

132. The register entry shall be renewed based on:

132.1. a court judgement;

132.2. a statement on the dissolution of marriage issued by a sworn notary;

132.3. a submission of a person and an opinion of the General Registry Office;

132.4. a submission of a person without an opinion of the General Registry Office;

132.5. an administrative act;

132.6. Cabinet decision.

133. Based on a court judgement, the register entry shall be supplemented if:

133.1. the paternity of the child has been determined;

133.2. the fact of paternity has been established;

133.3. the court has satisfied the claim by which the paternity assumption has been contested (it has been established that the child has not been born of his or her mother's husband) or recognised paternity has been declared annulled;

133.4. maternity has been determined or contested;

133.5. the register entry on the parents of a child or one parent of the child has been annulled;

133.6. adoption has been confirmed or cancelled;

133.7. marriage has been dissolved or declared annulled.

134. Based on a submission by a person and an opinion of the General Registry Office, the register entry shall be supplemented if:

134.1. the parents of a child whose fact of birth has been registered as a foundling have become known;

134.2. a fictitious entry on the father has been deleted (if birth of the child was registered in accordance with the procedures laid down in Section 58 of the USSR Marriage and Family Code, by completing the information on the father with the surname and ethnicity of the mother, and the given name of the father of the child and patronymic according to the instructions of the mother);

134.3. for a child under 15 years of age a religious name as the second name of the child is entered;

134.4. for a child under 15 years of age who is given two names, but is called by only one name, the name which is not used is deleted;

134.5. a child under the age of 15 changes his or her name (names) to any name (names) - once, if the name (names) of the child is/are difficult to pronounce, or it/they is/are not good-sounding;

134.6. for a child from the age of 15 and a person of legal age to whom in the entry of the Birth Register and the original copy of the birth certificate two names have been entered, one name which is not used is deleted if in other personal documents only one name has been entered;

134.7. the surname of a child is changed to the surname of the father or the mother after the conclusion of marriage of the parents, the dissolution of the marriage of the parents, the conclusion of marriage of the father or mother, the dissolution of the marriage of the father or mother, after the change of the surname of the father or mother;

134.8. surnames of the parents of a child are different, and based on an agreement between the parents or on a court decision, separate custody has been established for one parent, and the surname of the child is changed to the surname of the parent who exercises separate custody rights;

134.9. surnames of the parents of a child are different, and one of the parents has been deprived of custody of the child, and the surname of the child is changed to the surname of the parent who exercises custody rights;

134.10. information on one parent has been deleted, and the surname of the child is changed to the surname of the parent who exercises custody rights;

134.11. the person has undergone complete or partial change of sex, and the entry on the sex of the person is changed in accordance with the sex indicated in a medical certificate issued by a medical treatment institution or a medical practitioner, or another document confirming the change of sex. The form of the name (names) and surname shall be reproduced in accordance with the sex;

134.12. the ethnicity entry of a child is changed in accordance with Paragraph 76 of this Regulation if information on one of the parents has been deleted in the Birth Register of the child or the register has been supplemented with information on one of the parents. A change in the ethnicity entry shall require the consent of the child if he or she has reached 12 years of age;

134.13. the place of birth and the date of birth is changed for an adoptee in accordance with the initial entry in the Birth Register which is indicated prior to the approval of the adoption (if the adoption has been approved until 31 August 1993 on the basis of Article 112 of the SSSR Marriage and Family Code and with which the entry regarding the place of birth and the date of birth of the adoptee is amended);

134.14. the ethnicity entry of a child shall be changed to the ethnicity entry of the father or mother after the change of the ethnicity entry of the father or mother. A change in the ethnicity entry shall require the consent of the child if he or she has reached 12 years of age.

[*21 June 2016; 31 March 2020*]

135. When introducing supplements to an entry in the Birth Register of a child in the cases referred to in Sub-paragraphs 134.3, 134.4, 134.5, 134.7, 134.8, and 134.14 of this Regulation, consent of the parents is necessary.

[*23 April 2024*]

136. Based on a submission of a person without an opinion of the General Registry Office, the register entry shall be supplemented if:

136.1. fictitious entry on the father has been deleted (if birth of the child was registered in accordance with the procedures laid down in Section 58 of the USSR Marriage and Family Code, by completing the information on the father with the surname and ethnicity of the mother, and the given name of the father of the child and patronymic according to the instructions of the mother), and a submission for the recognition of paternity has been submitted at the same time;

136.2. paternity of the child has been recognised (Annex 14.<sup>1</sup>);

136.3. the parents and the child acquire a common surname when a mutual marriage is concluded between the parents of the child;

136.4. the name (names) and surname, that has (have) been entered in the register in dialect or a foreign language, shall be reproduced in accordance with the norms of the literary Latvian language for the orthography of personal names;

136.5. a person, when reproducing a personal name which has been entered in dialect or a foreign language, also wishes to retain the historical form of his or her surname or the original form in another language.

[*21 June 2016*]

137. Based on an administrative act, the register entry shall be supplemented if:

137.1. it is allowed to change the given name (names) and surname of a child;

137.2. it is allowed to change the ethnicity entry;

137.3. it is allowed to change the name (names) or surname (name (names) and surname) of a person who has undergone complete or partial change of sex. The entry on the sex of the person shall be changed in accordance with the sex indicated in a medical certificate issued by a medical treatment institution or a medical practitioner or another document confirming the change of sex. The name (names) or surname (name (names) and surname) shall be entered in accordance with the decision to permit the change of the name (names) or surname (name (names) and surname).

[*21 June 2016*]

138. An entry in the Death Register shall be supplemented based on a statement of the court or rehabilitation institutions, a medical certificate issued by a medical treatment institution or a medical practitioner, a decision of a statement institution or a court judgement.

139. An opinion on the supplementation of the register entry shall be drawn up in one copy and together with the case retained by the General Registry Office.

140. An opinion on the refusal to supplement the register entry shall be drawn up in two copies. One copy of the opinion shall be issued to the person who has submitted the submission for the supplementation of the register entry, the second copy shall be retained by the General Registry Office.

141. After introducing supplementation, the General Registry Office which supplemented the entry or the Ministry of Justice shall issue a new certificate if:

141.1. an entry in the Birth Register has been supplemented in the cases referred to in Sub-paragraphs 133.1, 133.2, 133.3, 133.4, 133.5, 133.6, Paragraphs 134, 136, and 137 of this Regulation;

141.2. the sex of a child has been changed, and therefore personal names in the Birth Register are corrected based on the sex.

## IX. Correction of Register Entries

144. The register entry may be corrected if it has been drawn up in the General Registry Office or the mission.

145. A correction shall be made in any register entry if:

145.1. the General Registry Office or the mission has made a clerical or spelling error, distortion or omission of the information or separate words, entered incorrect or inaccurate information;

145.2. the personal name shall be aligned (personal name form options specified in documents confirming the registration of civil status acts and personal identification documents shall be eliminated) in accordance with Paragraph 7 of this Regulation.

[*21 June 2016; 21 August 2018*]

146. The General Registry Office shall correct the register entry based on:

146.1. a submission of an interested person and an opinion of the General Registry Office;

146.2. an opinion of the General Registry Office without a submission of an interested person if the General Registry Office has established that it has made a spelling or orthography error when entering information in the Register of Natural Persons;

146.3. court judgment.

[*11 May 2021; Amendment to Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

147. The register entry based on an opinion shall be corrected by the General Registry Office which received a submission for the correction of the register entry.

148. If the mission has received a submission for the correction of the register entry which has been drawn up by the General Registry Office or the mission, the General Registry Office which retains the register entry to be corrected shall process the case regarding the correction of the register entry and correct the register entry accordingly.

149. The following documents shall be appended to a submission for the correction of the register entry:

149.1. a document confirming the registration of a civil status act, issued on the basis of the register entry to be corrected;

149.2. documents supporting the necessity of the correction;

149.3. [21 June 2016].

150. If the documents of the correction case contain conflicting or ambiguous information on a person's identity, the General Registry Office shall send the case to the territorial institution of the State Police in accordance with the place of residence of the submitter. The territorial institution of the State Police shall verify the personal data, and the information on the verification results shall be sent back to the General Registry Office together with the case.

151. If the General Registry Office establishes that there are grounds for making an analogue correction in another register entry, a submission of the family member of legal age to whom this entry refers shall be appended to the case, if he or she wishes to correct the error.

152. An opinion on correction of the register entry shall be drawn up in two copies. One copy of the opinion shall be issued to the person who has submitted the submission for the correction of the register entry, the second copy shall be retained by the General Registry Office.

153. An opinion on refusal to correct the register entry shall be drawn up in two copies. One copy shall be issued to the person who has submitted the submission for the correction of the register entry, the second copy shall be retained by the General Registry Office.

154. The General Registry Office which made the correction shall issue a certificate of a new content after making the corrections referred to in Paragraph 146 of this Regulation in the register entry.

## X. Cancelling of Register Entries

155. The register entry may be cancelled if it has been drawn up at the General Registry Office or the mission. The register entry shall be cancelled by the Ministry of Justice based on a court judgement or a decision of the Ministry of Justice.

156. The General Registry Office which has received a submission for cancelling the register entry shall process the case for cancelling the register entry.

157. The General Registry Office shall process the cancellation case at its own initiative in accordance with the procedures laid down by the Administrative Procedure Law if a document confirming registration of a civil status act has not been issued based on the repeatedly made register entry.

158. If the mission has received a submission for cancelling the register entry which has been drawn up by the General Registry Office, the General Registry Office which retains the register entry to be cancelled shall process the cancellation case of the register entry.

159. A submission for cancelling the registration entry shall be submitted by:

159.1. the person on whom the respective entry has been made or his or her authorised person;

159.2. a person who justifies his or her legal interests.

[*21 June 2016*]

160. The following documents shall be appended to the submission:

160.1. a document which confirms that the entry has been renewed or made repeatedly;

160.2. a document which certifies that the first entry has been preserved;

160.3. documents proving that the entries referred to in Sub-paragraphs 160.1 and 160.2 of this Regulation have been made on the submitter if the surname of the submitter in the personal identification document does not correspond to the surname in the first entry or in the renewed entry or in the entry made repeatedly;

160.4. an explanation for the reasons why the entry has been renewed or made repeatedly.

[*21 June 2016*]

161. In addition to the documents referred to in Paragraph 160 of this Regulation, the following documents shall be appended:

161.1. a list of biographical data of the submitter if the register entry to be cancelled concerns him or her;

161.2. documents proving that the submitter belongs to the deceased person's circle of heirs if the entry to be cancelled refers to the fact of the death of a person;

161.3. an explanation if the renewed and repeated register entry and the first entry contain conflicting information.

[*21 June 2016*]

162. The General Registry Office shall request other institutions to provide documents necessary for explaining the contradictions.

163. If the renewed and repeated register entry and the first entry contain conflicting or ambiguous information and there are doubts of the personal data of the submitter, the General Registry Office shall send the case to the territorial institution of the State Police in accordance with the place of residence of the submitter. The territorial institution of the State Police shall verify the personal data, and the information on the verification results shall be sent back to the General Registry Office together with the case.

[*21 June 2016*]

164. The General Registry Office shall send the case to the Ministry of Justice for taking a decision. The Ministry of Justice shall, in accordance with the procedures laid down in the Administrative Procedure Law, take the decision to cancel the entry or the refusal to cancel the entry.

165. The decision to cancel the entry or the refusal to cancel the entry shall be signed by the director of the Civil Registry Department of the Ministry of Justice. The decision shall be confirmed with a seal with the State coat of arms.

166. The decision to cancel the register entry shall be drawn up in two copies. One copy of the decision shall be issued to the person who submitted the submission for cancelling the register entry, but the other copy of the decision shall be retained by the Ministry of Justice.

167. The decision to refuse to cancel the register entry shall be drawn up in two copies. One copy of the decision and documents the person has appended to the submission shall be issued to the submitter, but the other copy of the decision shall be retained by the Ministry of Justice.

168. If a case for the cancellation of the register entry has been submitted by the General Registry Office at its own initiative, the decision to refuse to cancel the register entry shall be drawn up in two copies. One copy of the decision shall be retained by the General Registry Office which kept the cancellation case, and the other copy shall be retained by the Ministry of Justice.

169. The decision to cancel the register entry or the refusal to cancel the register entry shall be notified to the submitter in accordance with the procedures laid down in the Administrative Procedure Law.

## XI. Repeated Issue of Documents Confirming the Registration of Civil Status Acts

170. In order to repeatedly obtain a document, the person shall submit, send by post or to the official e-mail address of the institution or submit as an electronic document through the e-services of the portal of State administration services www.latvija.gov.lv a respective submission to the General Registry Office, the mission, or the Ministry of Justice. The following shall be indicated in the submission:

170.1. given name (names), surname, personal identity number, address of the place of residence, phone number or e-mail address;

170.2. justification of the necessity to repeatedly obtain the document;

170.3. depending on the type of document that needs to be obtained repeatedly - date and place of the conclusion of marriage, dissolution of marriage, birth, death, place and year of registration of the respective register entry;

170.4. personal data of the person for whom the repeated issue of the document is requested, and information on the person's relationship with the submitter.

[*23 April 2024*]

171. After receipt of the submission, the General Registry Office, the mission or the Ministry of Justice shall verify the fact of registration of the register entry in the Register of Natural Persons. If the register entry has been registered in the Register of Natural Persons, the General Registry Office, the mission or the Ministry of Justice shall repeatedly issue the requested document.

[*11 May 2021; Amendment to Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

172. If the register entry has not been registered in the Register of Natural Persons, the General Registry Office, the mission or the Ministry of Justice where the submission for the repeated obtaining of the documents has been submitted shall contact the General Registry Office which retains the register entry or the Ministry of Justice if the respective register entry has not been preserved by the General Registry Office. The General Registry Office which retains the register entry or the Ministry of Justice shall enter information on the register entry in the Register of Natural Persons.

[*11 May 2021; Amendment to Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

173. If neither the first nor the second copy of the register entry has been preserved or if the requested register entry cannot be found either at the General Registry Office or at the Ministry of Justice, the General Registry Office or the Ministry of Justice shall issue a relevant statement.

[*21 August 2018*]

174. The General Registry Office or the Ministry of Justice shall verify the respective type of registers for a period of three years before and three years after the year of registration of the register entry indicated in the submission. If the requested register is not found, it shall be verified whether the register entry has not been renewed.

175. In the document to be issued repeatedly, the date shall be indicated by adding 12 days to the date indicated in the register entry for all events that have taken place before 31 December 1900, and 13 days to all events from 1 January 1901 to 31 December 1917.

176. A document to be issued repeatedly which has been printed out from the Register of Natural Persons shall be issued in person to the person on whom the register entry has been drawn up, or to other persons indicated in the Law on Registration of Civil Status Acts.

[*11 May 2021; Amendment to Paragraph shall come into force on 28 June 2021. See Paragraph 2 of Amendments*]

177. If the submission has been received by post, the General Registry Office or the Ministry of Justice shall, upon request of the submitter, send the repeatedly issued document as a registered letter to the address indicated in the submission within one month.

[*23 April 2024*]

178. Documents shall be sent to foreign countries in accordance with the procedures laid down in the international agreements binding on the Republic of Latvia. Documents to foreign countries with which the Republic of Latvia has not concluded international agreements laying down the procedures for sending documents shall be sent with the intermediation of the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, if the document needs to be legalised.

EXTRACT FROM THE CIVIL LAW

Conclusion of Marriage

Section 41. A marriage shall be concluded not earlier than within a month and not later than within six months following the submission of a submission and other documents necessary for the conclusion of marriage to the General Registry Office if there are no impediments to the conclusion of marriage indicated in Sections 32, 35, 37 and 38 of this Law.

Section 45. In urgent cases, the official of the General Registry Office has the right to reduce the minimum period referred to in Section 41 of this Law.

Paragraph one of Section 56. A marriage shall be solemnised in the personal presence of the bride and the groom. The bride and the groom may invite one or two witnesses of the age of majority. The absence of witnesses shall not impede the conclusion of marriage.

Impediments to the Conclusion of Marriage

Section 32. Marriage prior to the reaching eighteen years of age is prohibited except in the case provided for in Section 33.

Section 33. By way of exception, a person who has reached sixteen years of age may marry with the consent of his or her parents or guardians if he or she marries a person of age of majority. If the parents or guardians, without good cause, refuse to give permission, then permission may be given by an Orphan's and Custody Court of the place where the parents or appointed guardians reside.

Section 35. Marriage is prohibited between kin in a direct line, brothers and sisters, and half-brothers and half-sisters (Section 213). Marriage between persons of the same sex is prohibited.

Section 37. Marriage between an adopter and an adoptee is prohibited.

Section 38. A new marriage for a person who is already married is prohibited. Similarly, marriage between a guardian with his or her ward, before the termination of the relations of guardianship, is prohibited.

EXTRACT FROM THE CIVIL LAW

Conclusion of Marriage

Section 41. A marriage shall be concluded not earlier than within a month and not later than within six months following the submission of a submission and other documents necessary for the conclusion of marriage to the General Registry Office if there are no impediments to the conclusion of marriage indicated in Sections 32, 35, 37 and 38 of this Law.

Section 45. In urgent cases, the official of the General Registry Office has the right to reduce the minimum period referred to in Section 41 of this Law.

Paragraph one of Section 56. A marriage shall be solemnised in the personal presence of the bride and the groom. The bride and the groom may invite one or two witnesses of the age of majority. The absence of witnesses shall not impede the conclusion of marriage.

Impediments to the Conclusion of Marriage

Section 32. Marriage prior to the reaching eighteen years of age is prohibited except in the case provided for in Section 33.

Section 33. By way of exception, a person who has reached sixteen years of age may marry with the consent of his or her parents or guardians if he or she marries a person of age of majority. If the parents or guardians, without good cause, refuse to give permission, then permission may be given by an Orphan's and Custody Court of the place where the parents or appointed guardians reside.

Section 35. Marriage is prohibited between kin in a direct line, brothers and sisters, and half-brothers and half-sisters (Section 213). Marriage between persons of the same sex is prohibited.

Section 37. Marriage between an adopter and an adoptee is prohibited.

Section 38. A new marriage for a person who is already married is prohibited. Similarly, marriage between a guardian with his or her ward, before the termination of the relations of guardianship, is prohibited.
