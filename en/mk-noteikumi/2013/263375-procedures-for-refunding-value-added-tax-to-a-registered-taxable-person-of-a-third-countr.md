# Procedures for Refunding Value Added Tax to a Registered Taxable Person of a Third Country or Third Territory

Issued pursuant to Section 112, Paragraph two
of the Value Added Tax Law

## I. General Provisions

1. This Regulation prescribes:

1.1. the period for which a registered taxable person of a third country or third territory may request a refund of the value added tax (hereinafter - the tax) paid for goods purchased and services received in the territory of the Republic of Latvia (hereinafter - inland) and for the importation of goods for ensuring economic activity outside the territory of the European Union, and also the minimum amount of tax to be refunded;

1.2. the procedures by which the State Revenue Service shall decide on the complete or partial refund of the tax amount indicated in an application of a registered taxable person of a third country or third territory (hereinafter - the application) or on the refusal to refund the tax, and the procedures by which the State Revenue Service shall refund the tax to a registered taxable person of a third country or third territory within the time limit laid down in Section 112, Paragraph 1<sup>1</sup> of the Value Added Tax Law;

1.3. the documents to be submitted to the State Revenue Service and time limits for submission;

1.4. the sample application form;

1.5. the cases when the State Revenue Service shall take the decision to refuse to refund the tax amount indicated in the application.

2. The procedures laid down in this Regulation shall be applied if a registered taxable person of a third country or third territory meets the conditions of Section 112, Paragraph one of the Value Added Tax Law, including:

2.1. during the period for which tax refund is requested (hereinafter - the refund period), the registered taxable person of a third country or third territory has not had a place of establishment of a business or a fixed establishment inland from which economic activity is carried out, or a declared place of residence, or a place of permanent residence;

2.2. during the refund period the registered taxable person of a third country or third territory has not supplied goods or services which can be regarded as an inland supply of goods and services, except:

2.2.1. the supply of goods transport services and services related to the transportation of goods if the tax for these services shall not be paid into the State budget in accordance with the laws and regulations in the field of value added tax in force in the Republic of Latvia;

2.2.2. the supply of goods and services to persons who are liable for the payment of the tax into the State budget in accordance with the laws and regulations in the field of value added tax in force in the Republic of Latvia;

2.2.3. the transactions carried out in accordance with the special tax arrangements laid down in Section 140.<sup>2</sup> or 140.<sup>4</sup> of the Value Added Tax Law.

[*9 January 2024*]

3. The refund period:

3.1. shall not be longer than one calendar year and not shorter than three calendar months;

3.2. may be shorter than three calendar months if these are the last months of the calendar year.

4. The total amount of the tax for which refund is requested shall not be less than:

4.1. EUR 400 if the refund period indicated in the application is shorter than one calendar year but not shorter than three calendar months;

4.2. EUR 50 if the refund period indicated in the application is one calendar year or is shorter than three calendar months, and these are the last months of a calendar year.

## II. Procedures by Which a Registered Taxable Person of a Third Country or Third Territory Submits the Application to the State Revenue Service

5. The tax shall be refunded on the basis of the application (Annex) submitted to the State Revenue Service by a registered taxable person of a third country or third territory or by its authorised person.

6. The application shall be submitted within the following time limits:

6.1. by 30 September of the calendar year following the refund period if the refund period is one calendar year;

6.2. within three months after the end of the refund period indicated in the application if:

6.2.1. the refund period is shorter than one calendar year but not shorter than three calendar months;

6.2.2. the refund period is shorter than three calendar months, and these are the last months of the calendar year.

7. If the application is sent by post, the date of submission shall be deemed to be the date on which the application has been handed over to the post office (postmark).

8. The application shall be completed in Latvian or English.

9. The authorised person of a registered taxable person of a third country or third territory may be a natural or a legal person who acts in accordance with one of the following powers of attorney:

9.1. a power of attorney issued and legalised in the issuing country, unless otherwise stipulated by the Document Legalisation Law;

9.2. a notarised power of attorney issued in a European Union Member State, a country of the European Economic Area, or in the Swiss Confederation;

9.3. a notarised power of attorney issued in the issuing country in conformity with the provisions of an agreement if the Republic of Latvia has concluded a bilateral or multilateral agreement with such country on legal assistance and legal relationships.

10. A registered taxable person of a third country or third territory or its authorised person shall certify by signing the application that:

10.1. the information in the application is true;

10.2. during the refund period there has been no economic activity inland and no taxable transactions performed, with the exception of the transactions referred to in Sub-paragraphs 2.2.1., 2.2.2, and 2.2.3. of this Regulation;

10.3. the applicant undertakes to repay any amount of tax into the State budget within three years if it is transferred in error to the bank account specified in the application.

[*9 January 2024*]

11. The following shall be attached to the application:

11.1. copies of the value added tax invoices (hereinafter - the tax invoice) paid for the goods purchased and services received inland during the refund period or copies of documents certifying the importation of goods during the refund period if:

11.1.1. the taxable value indicated in the tax invoice or the document certifying the importation of goods reaches or exceeds EUR 1000;

11.1.2. the tax invoice applies to fuel and the taxable value indicated in the tax invoice reaches or exceeds EUR 250;

11.2. [27 May 2025];

11.3. [27 May 2025];

11.4. a statement (original) issued by the tax authority of the relevant country, which shall be valid for 12 months from the date of issue thereof, certifying that during the refund period indicated in the application the person who has purchased goods or received services inland, or has imported goods, has been a registered taxable person outside the territory of the European Union;

11.5. if the application is submitted by an authorised person, the original of the power of attorney which meets the provisions of Paragraph 9 of this Regulation.

[*27 May 2025*]

## III. Procedures by Which the State Revenue Service Examines the Application and Decides on the Complete or Partial Refund of the Tax Amount Indicated in the Application or on the Refusal to Refund the Tax

12. The State Revenue Service shall verify whether the application and the information indicated therein meets the following conditions:

12.1. the application has been submitted within the time limit indicated in Paragraph 6 of this Regulation;

12.2. the refund period indicated in the application complies with the refund period indicated in Paragraph 3 of this Regulation;

12.3. the information has been provided in Latvian or English;

12.4. the total amount of tax for which a refund is requested is not less than:

12.4.1. EUR 400 if the refund is requested for the refund period referred to in Sub-paragraph 4.1 of this Regulation;

12.4.2. EUR 50 if the refund is requested for the refund period referred to in Sub-paragraph 4.2 of this Regulation;

12.5. the application includes a certification in accordance with Paragraph 10 of this Regulation;

12.6. the application provides information on each tax invoice issued inland or each document certifying the importation of goods inland;

12.7. documents have been attached to the application in accordance with Paragraph 11 of this Regulation;

12.8. the registered taxable person of a third country or third territory has not been registered in the State Revenue Service Value Added Tax Taxable Persons Register during the refund period indicated in the application.

13. If the information provided in the application and the documents attached thereto is sufficient to decide on the complete or partial refund of the tax amount indicated in the application or on the refusal to refund the tax, the State Revenue Service shall take such a decision within four months after receipt of the application.

14. The State Revenue Service shall take the decision to refuse to refund the tax if:

14.1. the application fails to meet at least one of the conditions of Paragraph 12 of this Regulation;

14.2. refund is requested:

14.2.1. for a transaction that has not taken place;

14.2.2. for an amount of tax indicated in the tax invoice which fails to meet the requirements of the Value Added Tax Law;

14.2.3. for the inland acquisition of immovable property not in use and for services received inland related to the construction, rebuilding, renewal, restoration of the immovable property, or the services referred to in point (k) of Article 31a(2) of Council Implementing Regulation (EU) No 282/2011 of 15 March 2011 laying down implementing measures for Directive 2006/112/EC on the common system of value added tax (recast);

14.2.4. for goods purchased and services received inland for personal use (for example, lease, maintenance or repair of a passenger car, purchase of fuel, lubricants and spare parts intended for a car, costs for recreational activities, catering services (including restaurant), health improvement activities and entertainment);

14.2.5. by a registered taxable person of a third country or third territory operating in accordance with the special taxation arrangement for tour operators and using the supply of goods and services inland for ensuring tourism services.

[*9 January 2024*]

14.<sup>1</sup> The condition referred to in Sub-paragraph 14.2.3 of this Regulation in respect of services received inland relating to the construction, rebuilding, renewal, restoration of immovable property, or the services referred to in point (k) of Article 31a(2) of Council Implementing Regulation (EU) No 282/2011 of 15 March 2011 laying down implementing measures for Directive 2006/112/EC on the common system of value added tax (recast) shall not apply if the refund is requested by a registered taxable person of a third country or third territory who uses the abovementioned services:

14.<sup>1</sup>1. for transactions in which the recipient of the services is liable for the payment of the tax in accordance with Section 89, Paragraph two of the Value Added Tax Law;

14.<sup>1</sup>2. for transactions carried out in accordance with the special tax arrangements laid down in Section 140.<sup>2</sup> or 140.<sup>4</sup> of the Value Added Tax Law.

[*9 January 2024*]

15. If the information provided in the application and the documents attached thereto is not sufficient to decide on the complete or partial refund of the tax amount indicated in the application or on the refusal to refund the tax, the State Revenue Service is entitled to request the applicant to provide additional information within four months after receipt of the application. Additional information shall be submitted within one month after the day on which the applicant has received the request to provide additional information.

15.<sup>1</sup> If the State Revenue Service has justified doubts about the validity of a particular application, it shall also request the original or a copy of the relevant tax invoice or the document certifying the importation of goods in addition to the information requested in accordance with Paragraph 15 of this Regulation. In such case, the threshold values referred to in Sub-paragraph 11.1 of this Regulation shall not be applied.

[*27 May 2025*]

16. If additional information is requested in accordance with Paragraph 15 of this Regulation, the State Revenue Service shall, within six months after receipt of the application, decide on the complete or partial refund of the tax or on the refusal to refund the tax.

17. If, within one month after receipt of the additional information requested in accordance with Paragraph 15 of this Regulation, the State Revenue Service requires more information to be submitted within one month after the date on which the applicant has received the request for repeated additional information, the State Revenue Service shall, within eight months after receipt of the application, decide on the complete or partial refund of the tax or on the refusal to refund the tax.

18. If the additional information requested in accordance with Paragraph 15 of this Regulation or the repeated additional information requested in accordance with Paragraph 17 of this Regulation is not received within one month after the day on which the applicant has received the request for additional information, the State Revenue Service shall take the decision to refuse to refund the tax.

19. If, in accordance with Paragraph 13 of this Regulation, the State Revenue Service has taken the decision to refuse to refund the tax on the basis of failure to meet the conditions provided in Sub-paragraphs 12.3., 12.5., 12.6 and 12.7. of this Regulation, the registered taxable person of a third country or third territory or its authorised person is entitled to repeatedly submit the application within one month after receipt of the decision.

20. If the application has been submitted repeatedly in accordance with Paragraph 19 of this Regulation:

20.1. the registered taxable person of a third country or third territory or its authorised person shall repeatedly send the application and the accompanying documents to the State Revenue Service, indicating in Paragraph V of the application form the date and number of the State Revenue Service decision, date of receipt of the decision and the documents that have been submitted repeatedly;

20.2. within one month after receipt of the application submitted repeatedly the State Revenue Service shall examine it and in accordance with Chapter III of this Regulation shall decide on the complete or partial refund of the tax or on the refusal to refund the tax.

## IV. Procedures for Tax Refund

21. [27 May 2025]

22. [27 May 2025]

23. [27 May 2025]

24. The approved amount of tax to be refunded shall be refunded within 10 working days after the State Revenue Service has decided on the complete or partial refund of the tax, but not later than within four months after receipt of the application. If the State Revenue Service has requested additional information, the approved amount of tax to be refunded shall be refunded within 10 working days after the State Revenue Service has decided on the complete or partial refund of the tax, but no later than six months after receipt of the application if the information was requested in accordance with Paragraph 15 of this Regulation, or within eight months if the information was requested in accordance with Paragraph 17 of this Regulation.

25. The approved amount of tax to be refunded shall be transferred to the bank account indicated in the application of the registered taxable person of a third country or third territory. The costs related to the bank transfer shall be deducted from the amount of tax to be refunded.

26. If the amount of tax has been transferred in error to the registered taxable person of a third country or third territory, it shall be repaid into the State budget.

27. The State Revenue Service is entitled to take a decision to reduce the amount of tax to be refunded in the subsequent refund periods by the amount of tax refunded in error, or take a decision to recover this amount from the registered taxable person of a third country or third territory in accordance with the law On Taxes and Fees.

28. If the amount of tax requested to be refunded has been obtained in a fraudulent manner, the State Revenue Service is entitled to recover from the registered taxable person of a third country or third territory, or from its authorised person, the unjustifiably paid amount and also the fine calculated in accordance with the laws and regulations in force in the Republic of Latvia governing criminal and administrative liability.

29. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs, upon a request of the State Revenue Service, shall provide information on the procedures for tax refund in the relevant country in conformity with the parity principle.

## V. Closing Provisions

30. Cabinet Regulation No. 27 of 8 January 2013, Procedures for Refunding Value Added Tax to a Registered Taxable Person of a Third Country or Third Territory (*Latvijas Vēstnesis*, 2013, 17, No. 208) is repealed.

31. Cabinet Regulation No. 1149 of 22 October 2013, Amendments to Cabinet Regulation No. 27 of 8 January 2013 Procedures for Refunding Value Added Tax to a Registered Taxable Person of a Third Country or Third Territory (*Latvijas Vēstnesis*, 2013, No. 208) is repealed.

32. This Regulation shall come into force on 1 January 2014.
