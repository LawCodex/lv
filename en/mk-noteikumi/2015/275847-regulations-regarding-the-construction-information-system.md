Issued pursuant to
Section 5, Paragraph one, Clause 6 of the Construction Law
and Section 114, Paragraph five of the law On Taxes and Fees

[*7 January 2020*]

## I. General Provisions

1. The Regulation prescribes the following for the Construction Information System (hereinafter - the System) which is managed by the State Construction Control Bureau (hereinafter - the manager of the System):

1.1. the procedures for establishing and maintaining the System;

1.2. the procedures for submitting documents;

1.3. the content and the procedures for the circulation of the information contained in the System.

[*3 January 2017*]

2. In order to ensure the administrative process of construction, to supervise the service of structures, to perform the functions of the management of residential houses and energy certification of buildings, and also to record electronic information on the construction site and to use it, the manager of the System shall ensure:

2.1. centralised obtaining of information for the keeping of the Register of Construction Merchants, the Register of Construction Specialists, the Register of Building Inspectors, the Register of Administrators of Residential Houses, the Register of Energy Certificates of Buildings, the Register of Independent Experts in the field of energy performance of buildings, the Register of Harmonised Standards for Construction Products, the Register of Non-Compliant Construction Products, the Register of Energy Auditors of Undertakings, and the Register of Energy Reports of Undertakings, for the provision of the administrative process of construction and the supervision of the service of structures, the keeping of the house file, and the taking of decisions of the community of apartment owners;

2.2. the standardisation of construction and service of structure documents and the requirements included therein;

2.3. the storage of construction and service of structure documents, the Register of Construction Merchants, the Register of Construction Specialists, the Register of Building Inspectors, the Register of Administrators of Residential Houses, the Register of Energy Certificates of Buildings, the Register of Independent Experts in the field of energy efficiency, the Register of Harmonised Standards for Construction Products, the Register of Non-Compliant Construction Products, the Register of Energy Auditors of Undertakings, and the Register of Energy Reports of Undertakings, and also the house files and the decisions taken by the community of apartment owners, documents and structured data in one place;

2.4. the provision of information to the public on the construction and service of structure processes, energy consumption of buildings, and administrators of residential houses which have obtained a professional qualification and which are carrying out professional activities in the administration of residential houses or are offering the services of an administrator;

2.5. the electronic services necessary for the public in the field of construction and service of structures, the energy efficiency of dwellings and buildings, and also the keeping of a house file, and the taking of decisions of the community of apartment owners;

2.6. establishment, maintenance of the unified electronic working time recording database, storage and provision of data, and also online access intended for competent authorities.

[*25 October 2022*]

3. The website address of the System is www.bis.gov.lv.

[*25 October 2022*]

4. The following participants shall be involved in the exchange of or access to the information to be included in the System:

4.1. the Ministry of Economics and institutions which are subordinate thereto;

4.2. sectoral ministries, i.e. the Ministry of Defence, the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of the Interior, the Ministry of Transport, the Ministry of Culture, the Ministry of Justice, the Ministry of Welfare, the Ministry of Environmental Protection and Regional Development, the Ministry of Agriculture, and institutions which are subordinate thereto;

4.3. local governments and institutions which are subordinate thereto;

4.4. authorities examining the competence of construction specialists;

4.5. independent experts in the area of energy performance of buildings;

4.6. the Public Utilities Commission;

4.7. providers of public utilities;

4.8. *valsts sabiedrība ar ierobežotu atbildību "Zemkopības ministrijas nekustamie īpašumi"* [State limited liability company Immovable Property of the Ministry of Agriculture];

4.9. *valsts akciju sabiedrība "Latvijas Valsts ceļi"* [State joint-stock company Latvian State Roads];

4.10. [3 January 2017];

4.11. other authorities to which the maintenance of or access of the relevant information has been delegated in laws and regulations;

4.12. principal performers of construction works who are obliged to ensure recording of working time on a construction site through the system for electronic working time recording and to provide data to be entered in the unified electronic working time recording database;

4.13. subcontractors who are obliged to use the system for electronic working time recording for recording working time on a construction site of the principal performer of construction work and to provide information to be entered in the unified electronic working time recording database;

4.14. persons employed on construction sites where the system for electronic working time recording is used for electronic working time recording;

4.15. the administrators of residential houses;

4.16. the owners and legal possessors of immovable properties.

[*3 January 2017; 7 January 2020; 25 October 2022*]

5. In using technical and logical solutions and information resources (software) integrated in the System, the System ensures availability, transfer, and exchange of information only to the extent to the persons, participants, and authorities that are entitled to receive and use it in accordance with the requirements of laws and regulations.

## II. Procedures for Establishing and Maintaining the System

6. In establishing the System, the manager thereof shall maintain an electronic environment for the support of the following processes, registers, operation of database, and electronic services:

6.1. the construction process, including initiation of construction, obtaining of a construction permit, circulation of construction documents, acceptance into the service of a structure and also the service of a structure;

6.2. the classification of construction merchants;

6.3. the Register of Construction Merchants;

6.4. the Register of Construction Specialists;

6.5. the Register of Building Inspectors;

6.6. the Register of Administrators of Residential Houses;

6.7. the Register of Independent Experts in the area of energy performance of buildings;

6.8. the unified electronic working time recording database;

6.9. the circulation of data on energy certification of buildings;

6.10. the Register of Harmonised Standards for Construction Products;

6.11. the Register of Non-Compliance of Construction Products;

6.12. the Register of Energy Auditors of Undertakings;

6.13. the Register of Energy Reports of Undertakings;

6.14. the administration of residential houses:

6.14.1. the keeping of a house file;

6.14.2. the taking of decisions of the community of apartment owners.

[*7 January 2020; 25 October 2022*]

7. The System database shall be kept permanently without transferring thereof to the National Archives of Latvia for storage.

8. If the System is eliminated, the database thereof shall be transferred to the National Archives of Latvia for storage in accordance with the laws and regulations in the field of State information systems and archives.

## III. Procedures for Submitting and Registering Documents on the System

9. A person (hereinafter - the System user) may submit documents in the System electronically, by completing the special online forms at www.bis.gov.lv, in accordance with Section 18, Paragraphs one and five of the Law on Administration of Residential Houses, the Law on the Energy Performance of Buildings, the laws and regulations in the field of construction in accordance with Section 5, Paragraph one of the Law on Administration of Residential Houses, and also keep a house file in accordance with the Law on Administration of Residential Houses and participate in the taking of decisions of the community of apartment owners in accordance with the Law on Residential Properties.

[*25 October 2022*]

10. Only identified System users shall be provided with access to special online forms and part of the System to which access is limited. The System shall provide a possibility to perform an identity check of the System user (authentication) by using the State Information System Integrator Authentication Module managed by the State Regional Development Agency. Foreigners who are not able to use the public authentication module of the State Information System integrator under responsibility of the State Regional Development Agency shall have access to the system with a user name and password issued by the building authority, the authority that performs the functions of the building authority, or the authority for the assessment of the competence of building specialists.

[*25 October 2022*]

11. If a payment is to be made for the receipt of a service, the System provides a possibility for the System user to make a payment for the service online by completing the special online forms referred to in Paragraph 9 of this Regulation and using the State Information System Integrator Authentication Module managed by the State Regional Development Agency.

12. The System user may:

12.1. sign documents with a secure electronic signature and a time stamp;

12.2. sign structured data and perform other activities on the System by using the electronic signature tool (the System tool) available in the electronic service of the System.

[*26 June 2018*]

12.<sup>1</sup> The System tool shall:

12.<sup>1</sup> 1. attest to the manipulations involving a data set that have been carried out by the System user;

12.<sup>1</sup> 2. attest to the time when the manipulations involving a set of data have been carried out (a time stamp);

12.<sup>1</sup> 3. records data which enable to ascertain that a set of data has not been changed since the moment of signature thereof.

[*26 June 2018*]

12.<sup>2</sup> The System user may request the manager of the System to issue an extract of the System data. Correctness of the extract of the System data shall be confirmed by a qualified electronic seal which is owned by the manager of the System within the meaning of Article 3(27) of Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 July 2014 on electronic identification and trust services for electronic transactions in the internal market and repealing Directive 1999/93/EC.

[*26 June 2018*]

12.<sup>3</sup> The System shall ensure sending of the decision to the official electronic address of a person if such has been established.

[*25 October 2022*]

13. The System shall ensure registration of a paper-based or electronic document by storing the following information about the document:

13.1. the date of the document;

13.2. the registration number of the document;

13.3. the type of the document;

13.4. the author of the document;

13.5. the addressee of the document;

13.6. the date of sending the document;

13.7. the start time of the sending (only for electronic documents);

13.8. the document file (if any);

13.9. the name of the document file (if there is a file);

13.10. the name of the document.

[*25 October 2022*]

14. The participants referred to in Paragraph 4 of this Regulation who are responsible for entering information in the System in accordance with laws and regulations shall ensure that the information specified in a document submitted in paper format is entered in the System.

14.<sup>1</sup> A notice about construction, an explanatory statement, a certification card, a construction work submission, and any other requests shall be completed in an online form as a type of structured data in the System, appending data thereto which contain documents or the annexes thereof and also information obtained from the State information systems.

[*25 October 2022*]

14.<sup>2</sup> Technical and special regulations shall be uploaded to the System in the form of data.

[*26 June 2018*]

## IV. Content of the System

15. The System shall contain the information referred to in Section 24, Paragraph two of the Construction Law, the unified electronic working time recording database, and the following registers and files:

15.1. the Register of Construction Merchants;

15.2. the Register of Construction Specialists;

15.3. the Register of Building Inspectors;

15.4. the Register of Independent Experts in the area of energy performance of buildings;

15.5. the Register of Energy Performance Certificates of Buildings;

15.6. the Register of Administrators of Residential Houses;

15.7. the Register of Harmonised Standards for Construction Products;

15.8. the Register of Non-Compliance of Construction Products;

15.9. the Register of Energy Auditors of Undertakings;

15.10. the Register of Energy Reports of Undertakings;

15.11. the construction file;

15.12. the service of a structure file;

15.13. the house file.

[*25 October 2022*]

15.<sup>1</sup> The construction file is a digital collection created in the System for ensuring the administrative process of construction in which the data, documents, and files related to the implementation of a specific construction project are reflected or stored. Access to the construction file shall be for persons to whom such rights are provided for in the laws and regulations in the field of construction or in the field of disclosure of information, and also for State administrative bodies for the performance of their functions.

[*25 October 2022*]

15.<sup>2</sup> The service of a structure file is a digital collection created in the System for ensuring the administrative process of the supervision of the service of a structure in which the data, documents, and files related to the service of the specific structure are reflected or stored. Access to the service of a structure file shall be for persons to whom such rights are provided for in the laws and regulations in the field of construction or in the field of disclosure of information, and also for State administrative bodies for the performance of their functions.

[*25 October 2022*]

16. The volume and content of the data to be included in the registers and files referred to in the unified electronic working time recording database and in Paragraph 15 of this Regulation shall be determined in accordance with the laws and regulations in the field of construction, service of structures, energy performance of dwellings and buildings which govern the operation of registers and files, the law On Taxes and Fees, and the laws and regulations issued on the basis thereof.

[*25 October 2022*]

17. For the purpose of ensuring compliance of the information referred to in Paragraph 15 of this Regulation with the submitted data, updating, rectification thereof, and also data quality:

17.1. the information referred to in Sub-paragraphs 15.2, 24.1, 24.2.2, Paragraph 30, Sub-paragraphs 31.1.3, 31.1.4, 31.2, Paragraphs 32.<sup>1</sup>, 32.<sup>2</sup>, 40, and 41 of this Regulation shall be used in the Register of Construction Merchants;

17.2. the information referred to in Sub-paragraph 24.1.1, Paragraph 28, 40, and 41 of this Regulation shall be used in the Register of Construction Specialists;

17.3. the information referred to in Sub-paragraphs 15.2, 24.1, 24.2.2 and Paragraph 28 of this Regulation shall be used in the Register of Building Inspectors;

17.4. the information referred to in Sub-paragraphs 15.2, 24.1.1, Paragraphs 28, 40, and 41 of this Regulation shall be used in the Register of Independent Experts in the area of energy performance of buildings;

17.5. the information referred to in Sub-paragraphs 24.1, 24.2.1.6, 24.2.1.7, 24.2.1.11, Paragraphs 28 and 30 of this Regulation shall be used in the Register of Energy Performance Certificates of Buildings;

17.6. the information referred to in Sub-paragraphs 24.1, 24.2, Paragraphs 26, 28, 30, Sub-paragraphs 31.1.3 and 31.1.4 of this Regulation shall be used in the Register of Administrators of Residential Houses;

17.7. the manager of the System, building authorities, or authorities performing the functions of a building authority shall use the information referred to in Sub-paragraphs 15.1, 15.2, 15.3, 15.4, 15.5, Paragraphs 24, 25, 26, 27, 28, 30, 32, 40, and 41 of this Regulation;

17.8. the information referred to in Sub-paragraphs 15.1, 24.2.1.1, 26.1.7, Paragraph 27, Sub-paragraphs 28.1, 28.2, 28.4, Paragraphs 30 and 31 of this Regulation shall be used in the unified electronic working time recording database;

17.9. the information referred to in Paragraphs 28 and 30 of this Regulation shall be used in the Register of Energy Auditors of Undertakings;

17.10. the information referred to in Paragraphs 28 and 30 of this Regulation shall be used in the Register of Energy Reports of Undertakings;

17.11. the information referred to in Sub-paragraphs 15.1, 15.2, 15.3, 15.5, Paragraphs 24, 25, 26, 27, 28, 30, 32, 32.<sup>3</sup>, and 32.<sup>4</sup> of this Regulation shall be used in the construction file;

17.12. the information referred to in Sub-paragraphs 15.2, 15.5, 15.6 and Paragraphs 24, 26, 28, and 30 of this Regulation shall be used in a service of a structure file;

17.13. the information referred to in Sub-paragraphs 15.5, 24.2.1.1, 24.2.1.2, and 24.2.1.5 of this Regulation shall be used in a house file.

[*3 January 2017; 9 October 2018; 7 January 2020; 31 August 2021; 25 October 2022*]

19.1. the cadastral designation of the unit of land;

19.2. the address of the unit of land if such has been allocated;

19.3. the given name, surname, personal identity number of the owner of the unit of land or, if there is no owner, of the legal possessor and user of the unit of land or the name and unified registration number of a legal person;

19.5. any other information in accordance with the requirements of the laws and regulations governing construction.

20. [9 October 2018]

22.4. the type of construction of the group of premises;

22.10. other information in accordance with the requirements of the laws and regulations governing construction and the service of structures.

[*25 October 2022*]

23. The information referred to in Section 24, Paragraph two of the Construction Law and Paragraphs 18, 19, 21, and 22 of this Regulation shall be entered in the System (unless it is received from other State information systems) within a period from the initiation of construction to the acceptance of a structure for service according to the relevant stage of the construction process.

[*9 October 2018*]

24.1. with the following textual and geospatial information from the State Address Register Information System online:

24.1.1. data from the classifier of addresses with coordinates of address points and spatial and textual linking data;

24.1.2. borders of administrative territories and territorial units (for the territory of Latvia);

24.2. with the following information from the Immovable Property State Cadastre Information System online:

24.2.1. textual data which have been structured in the following sets of information:

24.2.1.1. identification of the cadastre object;

24.2.1.2. composition of the immovable property;

24.2.1.3. data about the name and total area of the immovable property;

24.2.1.4. registration of the right to the immovable property;

24.2.1.5. basic data about the ownership and lessees of the immovable property;

24.2.1.6. basic data about the unit of land;

24.2.1.7. basic data about the part of the unit of land;

24.2.1.8. purpose of use of the immovable property of the unit of land;

24.2.1.9. purpose of use of the immovable property of the part of the unit of land;

24.2.1.10. encumbrances of the immovable property object;

24.2.1.11. basic data about the structure;

24.2.1.12. type of the structure;

24.2.1.13. structural elements of the structure;

24.2.1.14. indicators of scope of the structure;

24.2.1.15. division of the total area of the structure by types of area;

24.2.1.16. division of the total area of the group of premises by types of area;

24.2.1.17. basic data about the group of premises;

24.2.1.18. basic data about premises (division of the total area of the group of premises by types of premises and characteristic indicators of premises);

24.2.1.19. registered improvements of the structure;

24.2.1.20. registered improvements of the group of premises;

24.2.1.21. basic data about the lease;

24.2.1.22. notations (prohibitions of alienation);

24.2.1.23. information about cadastral survey of the unit of land;

24.2.1.24. information about cadastral survey of the part of the unit of land;

24.2.1.25. information about the planned (designed) unit of land;

24.2.2. spatial data about the territory of Latvia;

24.2.3. information about the structures removed from the cadastre:

24.2.3.1. the cadastral designation of the structure;

24.2.3.2. the date when the structure was removed from the cadastre;

24.3. with the data about the restricted territories and objects to which protection zones are determined from the Information System of Restricted Territories online;

24.6. highly-detailed topographical information from the Central Database of Highly-detailed Topographical Information.

[*9 October 2018; 31 August 2021; 25 October 2022*]

24.<sup>1</sup> The manager of the System shall ensure that a building authority or an authority performing the functions of a building authority may access the data of the Information System of Restricted Territories referred to in Sub-paragraph 24.3 of this Regulation in a browsable manner (view mode), while an issuer of technical or special regulations may access the data about the territory to which a construction intention refers.

[*9 October 2018*]

24.<sup>2</sup> The manager of the System shall, in accordance with the conditions of the memorandum of understanding entered into with the State Land Service, ensure that the cadastral survey files of structures and groups of premises accumulated in the digital document storage of the State Land Service and referred to in Sub-paragraph 24.4 of this Regulation:

24.<sup>2</sup> 1. may be accessed in a browsable manner (view mode) by a building authority or an authority performing the functions of a building authority in respect of the structure or group of premises necessary for a specific construction process, and also by a local government for the purpose of ensuring rule of law of the construction and service processes within its administrative territory;

[*31 August 2021*]

25.1. structured data about the functional zones specified in the spatial plan, local plan, and amendments thereto, the functional zones according to topographic underlay, the sub-zones, the territories with special regulations, and also structured data about the regulations for the use and building of the territory;

26.4. information about encumbrances of the immovable property contained in part III of the division of the Land Register;

26.5. information about the debts of the immovable property contained in part IV of the division of the Land Register;
