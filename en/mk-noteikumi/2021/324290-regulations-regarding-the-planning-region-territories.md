# Regulations Regarding the Planning Region Territories

Republic of Latvia

**Cabinet
Regulation No. 418**
Adopted 22 June 2021

Issued pursuant to
Section 5, Paragraph two of the Regional Development Law

1. The Regulation prescribes the planning region territories.

2. The following is included in the planning region territories:

2.1. Kurzeme Planning Region:

2.1.1. Liepāja;

2.1.2. Ventspils;

2.1.3. Dienvidkurzeme municipality;

2.1.4. Kuldīga municipality;

2.1.5. Saldus municipality;

2.1.6. Talsi municipality;

2.1.7. Tukums municipality;

2.1.8. Ventspils municipality;

2.2. Latgale Planning Region:

2.2.1. Daugavpils;

2.2.2. Rēzekne;

2.2.3. Augšdaugava municipality;

2.2.4. Balvi municipality;

2.2.5. Krāslava municipality;

2.2.6. Līvāni municipality;

2.2.7. Ludza municipality;

2.2.8. Preiļi municipality;

2.2.9. Rēzekne municipality;

2.3. Rīga Planning Region:

2.3.1. Rīga;

2.3.2. Jūrmala;

2.3.3. Ādaži municipality;

2.3.4. Ķekava municipality;

2.3.5. Mārupe municipality;

2.3.6. Olaine municipality;

2.3.7. Ropaži municipality;

2.3.8. Salaspils municipality;

2.3.9. Sigulda municipality;

2.4. Vidzeme Planning Region:

2.4.1. Alūksne municipality;

2.4.2. Cēsis municipality;

2.4.3. Gulbene municipality;

2.4.4. Limbaži municipality;

2.4.5. Madona municipality;

2.4.6. Ogre municipality;

2.4.7. Saulkrasti municipality;

2.4.8. Smiltene municipality;

2.4.9. Valmiera municipality;

2.4.10. Valka municipality;

2.4.11. Varakļāni municipality;

2.5. Zemgale Planning Region:

2.5.1. Jelgava;

2.5.2. Aizkraukle municipality;

2.5.3. Bauska municipality;

2.5.4. Dobele municipality;

2.5.5. Jelgava municipality;

2.5.6. Jēkabpils municipality.

3. Cabinet Regulation No. 391 of 5 May 2009, Regulations Regarding the Planning Region Territories (*Latvijas Vēstnesis*, 2009, No. 72; 2011, No. 52), is repealed.

4. The Regulation shall come into force on 1 July 2021.

Prime Minister A. K. Kariņš

Minister for Environmental Protection
