# Price List of the Public Paid Services of the State Agency Civil Aviation Agency

Republic of Latvia

**Cabinet
Regulation No. 891**
Adopted 21 December 2021

Issued pursuant to
Section 5, Paragraph nine of the Law on Budget
and Financial Management and Section 5,
Paragraph one of the Public Agencies Law

1. The Regulation prescribes:

1.1. the price list of the public paid services provided by the State agency Civil Aviation Agency (hereinafter - the Civil Aviation Agency);

1.2. the payment procedures for the public paid services provided by the Civil Aviation Agency, the conditions of the application of rates and reliefs (reduction of the price).

2. The Civil Aviation Agency shall provide the public paid services in accordance with Annex to this Regulation.

3. A price reduction is applied to the services referred to in Sub-paragraphs 1.1.2, 1.4.3.3, 1.5.3, 2.1.1, 2.1.2, 2.1.3, 2.1.4, 2.1.5, 2.1.6, 2.1.7, 2.1.8, 2.1.9, 2.1.10, 2.1.11, 2.1.12, 2.1.13, 2.1.14, 2.1.15, 2.1.16, 2.1.17, 2.1.18, 2.2.1, 2.2.2, 2.2.3, 2.3.1, 2.3.2, 2.3.3, 2.3.4, 2.4.1, 2.4.2, 2.4.3, 2.4.4, 2.4.5, 2.4.6, 2.4.7, 2.4.8, 2.4.9, 2.5.1, 2.5.2, 5.2, 5.4, 5.6, 5.8, 6.3, 7.2.1, 7.2.2, 7.2.3, 7.2.4, 8.1.1, 8.1.2, 8.1.4, 8.1.5, 8.1.6, 8.1.7, 8.1.8, 8.1.9, 8.2, 9.1, 9.2, 10.6, 10.7, 12.5, 12.6, 13.2, 13.3, 13.4, 13.5, 13.6, 13.7, 13.9, 13.11, 14.1, 14.2, 14.3, 14.4, 16.1, 16.2, 17.1, 17.2, 17.3, 17.4, 17.5, 18.1, 18.2, 20.2.1, 20.2.2, 20.4, 20.4.1, 20.5, 20.6.1, 20.6.2, 20.6.3, 20.7.1, 20.7.2, 21.3.7, 21.5, 21.6, 21.12, 21.13, 24.1, 24.2, 24.3, 24.4, 24.5, Chapter VI, and Sub-paragraph 34.1 of Annex to this Regulation:

3.1. in 2022 - 76.92 % of the price;

3.2. in 2023 - 88.46 % of the price.

4. The services which are not referred to in Annex to this Regulation and which are ensured on the basis of the laws and regulations that have come into force after the coming into force of this Regulation shall be provided by the Civil Aviation Agency according to the hourly costs of human resources in the amount of EUR 43.26 per hour.

5. 5955 hours of human resources per year shall be applied to the service referred to in Sub-paragraph 10.2.1 of Annex to this Regulation.

6. The customer shall settle the accounts for a paid service before receiving the paid service.

7. The payment for the provided services shall be made in one of the following ways:

7.1. with a payment card at the payment card acceptance terminal at the Civil Aviation Agency;

7.2. through such payment service provider which has the right to provide payment services within the meaning of the Law on Payment Services and Electronic Money.

8. If the Civil Aviation Agency has started the fulfilment of the service but the service cannot be fulfilled due to the fault of the customer or the customer withdraws from the service, including before the end of the deadline for the provision of the service, the fee for the service shall not be reimbursed.

9. Cabinet Regulation No. 999 of 24 September 2013, Regulations Regarding the Price List of the Public Paid Services Provided by the State Agency Civil Aviation Agency (*Latvijas Vēstnesis*, 2013, No. 202; 2020, No. 248), is repealed.

10. The Regulation shall come into force on 1 January 2022.

Prime Minister A. K. Kariņš

Minister for Transport T. Linkaits
