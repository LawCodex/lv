# Regulations for the Registration and Supervision of Service Providers of the Electronic Road Toll System of the Road User Charge and Exclusion Thereof from the Register

Republic of Latvia

**Cabinet
Regulation No. 136**
Adopted 22 February 2022

Issued pursuant to Section 5.<sup>1</sup>of the Law on the Road User Charge

1. The Regulation prescribes the procedures for registering and supervising service providers of the electronic road toll system of the road user charge (hereinafter - the charge) as well as the procedures by which the service provider shall be excluded from the register.

2. Terms used in the Regulation:

2.1. electronic road toll system - a road toll system which automatically detects the presence of a vehicle in a certain location through remote communication with on-board equipment in the vehicle or automatic number plate recognition;

2.2. European Electronic Toll Service (hereinafter - EETS) - the toll service provided under a contract on one or more EETS domains by an EETS provider to an EETS user;

2.3. toll service - the service that enables users to use a vehicle in one or more EETS domains under a single contract and, where necessary, with one piece of on-board equipment, and which includes:

2.3.1. where necessary, providing a customised on-board equipment to users and maintaining its functionality;

2.3.2. guaranteeing that the toll charger is paid the toll due by the user;

2.3.3. providing to the user the means by which the payment is to be made or accepting an existing one;

2.3.4. collecting the toll from the user;

2.3.5. managing customer relations with the user;

2.3.6. implementing and adhering to the security and privacy policies for the road toll systems;

2.4. toll service provider - a legal entity providing toll services on one or more EETS domains for one or more classes of vehicles;

2.5. interoperability constituent - any elementary component, group of components, subassembly or complete assembly of equipment incorporated or intended to be incorporated into EETS upon which the interoperability of the service depends directly or indirectly, including both tangible objects and intangible objects such as software;

2.6. on-board equipment - the complete set of hardware and software components to be used as part of the toll service which is installed or carried on board a vehicle in order to collect, store, process, and remotely receive and transmit data, either as a separate device or embedded in the vehicle.

3. A merchant which wishes to register as an electronic road toll system service provider (hereinafter - the merchant) shall submit the following to *valsts sabiedrība ar ierobežotu atbildību "Latvijas Valsts ceļi"* [State limited liability company Latvian State Roads]:

3.1. a submission indicating the following:

3.1.1. the name, legal address, registration number of the merchant or the registration number and registration date of the branch of a foreign merchant with the Commercial Register;

3.1.2. the telephone number and electronic mail address of the merchant;

3.2. EN ISO 9001 certificate regarding the quality management system of the merchant. If the merchant has another certificate, it must prove the equivalence of such quality assurance certificate with the EN ISO 9001 certificate;

3.3. information proving that the merchant has the technical equipment and the European Commission declaration or certificate attesting the conformity of the interoperability constituents to specifications at its disposal;

3.4. information (detailed description of experience of the merchant) proving that the merchant has knowledge of the electronic road toll system service or other relevant domains (for example, the domain of banking, telecommunications, or telematic systems);

3.5. information regarding the services provided in the domain of electronic road toll system services or other relevant domain, characterising each type of service, the activities covered, and the description of the system used;

3.6. the financial statement of the last year of the merchant;

3.7. a certification that the merchant does not have tax or fee debts exceeding EUR 150;

3.8. the risk management plan to be audited at least every two years, indicating therein:

3.8.1. the means for evaluating and reducing the risks of the electronic road toll system;

3.8.2. a list of the identified, assessed, and grouped risks;

3.8.3. the planned measures and action strategy in order to avoid the identified risks or to reduce their impact, particularly in economy, financial, and technical field;

3.8.4. the activities to be performed in restricted financial circumstances.

4. The State limited liability company Latvian State Roads shall register the merchant as the electronic road toll system service provider if:

4.1. the merchant has submitted the information referred to in Paragraph 3 of this Regulation;

4.2. the total financial turnover of the merchant is at least EUR 500 000 (according to the data of the last annual statement);

4.3. the liquidity coefficient of the merchant is equivalent to 1 or more (according to the data of the last annual statement);

4.4. the own funds of the merchant are positive (according to the data of the last annual statement);

4.5. according to the information available in the database of tax (fee) debtors administered by the State Revenue Service, the merchant does not have tax or fee debts exceeding EUR 150;

4.6. there is no information in the Punishment Register regarding:

4.6.1. pending criminal record of the merchant or members of the executive board or supervisory board of the merchant in Latvia or other European Union Member States for criminal offences of an economic nature and illegal activities involving personal data of natural persons;

4.6.2. pending administrative penalties of the merchant or members of the executive board or supervisory board of the merchant in Latvia or other European Union Member States for administrative offences in relation to evasion of tax payments and payments equivalent thereto.

5. In order to register and supervise the electronic road toll system service providers, the State limited liability company Latvian State Roads shall obtain the following information:

5.1. from the Enterprise Register of the Republic of Latvia - the registration data of the merchant with the Commercial Register;

5.2. from the Information Centre of the Ministry of the Interior in the form of a statement - the information referred to in Sub-paragraph 4.6 of this Regulation from the Punishment Register.

6. The State limited liability company Latvian State Roads shall, once a year after registration, request the registered electronic road toll system service providers to submit the information specified in Sub-paragraphs 3.2, 3.6, 3.7, and 3.8. of this Regulation within a month and shall check the information referred to in Sub-paragraphs 4.2, 4.3, 4.4, 4.5, and 4.6 of this Regulation.

7. If the information obtained in accordance with Paragraph 6 of this Regulation on the merchant does not conform to the requirements of this Regulation, the State limited liability company Latvian State Roads shall send a request to the electronic road toll system service provider, imposing an obligation to eliminate, within one month, the non-conformities which have occurred and to submit the relevant information on the conformity of the electronic road toll system service provider with the requirements of this Regulation.

8. If, within one month after receipt of the request referred to in Paragraph 7 of this Regulation, the electronic road toll system service provider has not submitted information on the elimination of non-conformities or the information provided is incomplete, the State limited liability company Latvian State Roads shall take the decision to exclude the electronic road toll system service provider from the register.

9. The State limited liability company Latvian State Roads shall exclude the electronic road toll system service provider from the register upon its own wish if it has submitted a respective submission to the State limited liability company Latvian State Roads.

10. Cabinet Regulation No. 140 of 24 March 2015, Regulations Regarding Registration and Supervision of Service Providers of the Electronic Toll Service of the Road User Charge and Exclusion Thereof from the Register (*Latvijas Vēstnesis*, 2015, No. 66), is repealed.

## Informative Reference to the European Union Directive

The Regulation contains legal norms arising from Directive (EU) 2019/520 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 March 2019 on the interoperability of electronic road toll systems and facilitating cross-border exchange of information on the failure to pay road fees in the Union.
