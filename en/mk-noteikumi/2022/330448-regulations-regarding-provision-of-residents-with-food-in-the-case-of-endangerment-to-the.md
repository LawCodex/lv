# Regulations Regarding Provision of Residents with Food in the Case of Endangerment to the State

Republic of Latvia

**Cabinet
Regulation No. 147**
Adopted 1 March 2022

Issued pursuant to
Section 9, Clause 6
of the Mobilisation Law

1. The Regulation prescribes:

1.1. the norms for the provision of residents with food in the case of endangerment to the State;

1.2. the procedures for planning the provision of residents with food and also the issuance and control of issuance of food in the case of endangerment to the State;

1.3. the tasks and obligations of State and local government authorities in the provision of residents with food in the case of endangerment to the State.

2. The Regulation prescribes the daily norms for the following groups of residents in the case of endangerment to the State:

2.1. the civilians displaced (evacuated) within the country;

2.2. the persons staying entirely under the care of the State or local government in State or local government institutions, and the personnel of such institutions;

2.3. the personnel taking measures to eliminate consequences of the endangerment to the State.

3. The Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs shall, each year by 15 March, submit to the Ministry of Agriculture the data from the Register of Natural Persons on the number of residents by age groups and the distribution thereof across administrative territories in accordance with the data of the State information system as of 1 January of the respective year (Annex 1).

4. The Ministry of Agriculture shall, in cooperation with local governments, organise the planning of the provision with food in the case of endangerment to the State.

5. A local government shall ensure the issuance of food:

5.1. according to the daily norms specified for one resident (Annex 2);

5.2. following hygiene requirements during the issuance of food.

6. If any of the food products referred to in Annex 2 to this Regulation are not available, another food product as equivalent as possible may be offered.

7. The Food and Veterinary Service shall control:

7.1. the safety and quality of food products;

7.2. the compliance with hygiene requirements during food storage, transportation, and issuance.

8. A local government shall determine the procedures for ensuring issuance of food to residents within the administrative territory of the local government in the case of endangerment to the State. The issuance of food shall be coordinated by the civil protection commission of the cooperation territory of local governments.

9. In the case of endangerment to the State, a local government shall:

9.1. inform residents of the procedures for the issuance of food and the points of issuance through mass media;

9.2. approve the managers of the points of issuance of food and the required number of employees at the point of issuance of food;

9.3. organise the accounting of the food issued;

9.4. organise transport for food supply to residents, if necessary;

9.5. if necessary, organise transport to ensure water supply for residents, taking into account the local drinking water resources available to the local government.

10. The Ministry of Agriculture shall, in cooperation with local governments:

10.1. plan and coordinate deferred food supplies in accordance with the laws and regulations regarding deferred supplies;

10.2. conclude contracts with merchants for the production and supply of specific types of goods, based on an individual decision of the Cabinet;

10.3. plan and develop a mobilisation request for merchants to produce specific types of food products.

11. The Ministry of Agriculture shall, in accordance with the procedures laid down in the law On Local Governments, inform the Ministry of Environmental Protection and Regional Development of the violations established in the operation of local governments while carrying out the tasks specified in this Regulation.

12. Cabinet Regulation No. 585 of 28 August 2007, Regulations Regarding Provision of Residents with Food in the Case of Endangerment to the State (*Latvijas Vēstnesis*, 2007, No. 141; 2010, No. 16), is repealed.

## Daily Norms

I. For the civilians displaced (evacuated) within the country, the persons being entirely under the care of the State or local government in State or local government institutions, and the personnel of such institutions

| No. | Product | Quantity<br> (in grams or litres) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. | Infant and toddler formula1 | 200 g |
| 2. | Rolled cereals, flour, groats, instant potato mash, or pasta | 200-300 g |
| 3. | Canned meat or fish | 170-250 g |
| 4. | Bread, crispbread, or rusks | 150 g (bread)<br>40 g (rusks) |
| 5. | Egg powder, milk powder, peas, or other legumes | 20 g |
| 6. | Drinking water | 3 l (in winter period) and 4 l (in summer period) |

II. For the personnel taking measures to eliminate consequences of the endangerment to the State

| No. | Product | Quantity<br> (in grams or litres) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. | Rolled cereals, flour, groats, instant potato mash, or pasta | 200-300 g |
| 2. | Canned meat or fish | 170-250 g |
| 3. | Bread, crispbread, or rusks | 150 g (bread)<br>40 g (rusks) |
| 4. | Egg powder, milk powder, peas, or other legumes | 20 g |
| 5. | Canned vegetables | 100 g |
| 6. | Dried fruit and berries | 50 g |
| 7. | Jam | 20 g |
| 8. | Chocolate with cocoa content up to 70 % | 50 g |
| 9. | Coffee or cocoa | 20 g |
| 10. | Tea | 6 g |
| 11. | Sugar | 50 g |
| 12. | Drinking water | 3 l (in winter period) and 4 l (in summer period) |

Notes.
1. Depending on the availability of the product and the packaging weight, the norm of the respective product may be issued for one or multiple days.
2. <sup>1</sup> For children from birth up to the age of 2 years.
