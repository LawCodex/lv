# Price List of the Paid Services of the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs

| Text consolidated by Valsts valodas centrs (State Language Centre) with amending regulations of:<br>14 October 2025 [shall come into force from 20 October 2025].<br>If a whole or part of a paragraph has been amended, the date of the amending regulation appears in square brackets at the end of the paragraph. | --- |
| <br> Republic of Latvia<br>**Cabinet<br> Regulation No. 515<br>** Adopted 12 September 2023 |

Issued pursuant to Section 5, Paragraph nine
of the Law on Budget and Financial Management

1. This Regulation prescribes the price list of the paid services of the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs.

2. The Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs shall provide paid services in accordance with the price list (Annex).

3. Fee for a paid service of the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs shall be paid into the basic budget account opened with the Treasury by the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs.

4. Cabinet Regulation No. 562 of 19 September 2017, Price List of the Paid Services of the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs (*Latvijas Vēstnesis*, 2017, No. 188), is repealed.

Prime Minister, Acting for the Minister for Foreign Affairs A. K. Kariņš

Minister for the Interior M. Kučinskis
