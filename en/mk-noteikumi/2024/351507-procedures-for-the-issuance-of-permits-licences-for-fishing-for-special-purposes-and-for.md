# Procedures for the Issuance of Permits (Licences) for Fishing for Special Purposes and for Scientific Research Purposes

Republic of Latvia

**Cabinet
Regulation No. 250**
Adopted 23 April 2024

Issued pursuant to
Section 12, Paragraph four
of the Fishery Law

1. The Regulation prescribes the procedures for the issuance of permits (licences) for fishing for special purposes and for scientific research purposes (hereinafter - the permit (licence)) in the inland waters of the Republic of Latvia (hereinafter - the inland waters), the Gulf of Riga of the Baltic Sea, or the territorial waters or the economic zone waters of the Republic of Latvia in the Baltic Sea (hereinafter - the Gulf of Riga or the Baltic Sea).

2. The permits (licences) shall be issued by the State Environmental Service (hereinafter - the Service).

3. A person who plans to fish for special purposes or for scientific research purposes shall submit to the Service a submission for obtaining the permit (licence).

4. If fishing for special purposes or for scientific research purposes is intended in the inland waters or the coastal waters of the Gulf of Riga or the Baltic Sea, the person shall indicate the following in the submission:

4.1. the name, registration number, address, and telephone number of the recipient of the permit (licence);

4.2. the given name, surname, and telephone number of the representative responsible for the organisation of scientific work;

4.3. the given name, surname, and telephone number of the representative responsible for fishing activities or fishing for special purposes;

4.4. the given name and surname of the fisherman (fishermen);

4.5. the period during which fishing is intended;

4.6. the location where fishing is intended. If fishing is intended in the inland waters, the body of water or part of the body of water and the local government (local governments) in the administrative territory of which it is located shall be indicated. If fishing is intended in the coastal waters of the Gulf of Riga or the Baltic Sea, the fishing location and the local government (local governments) to the administrative territory of which the coastal waters are adjacent shall be indicated;

4.7. the type, number, or length in metres of the intended fishing gear and the mesh size in millimetres;

4.8. the registration number of the vessel (vessels) to be used for fishing.

5. If fishing for special purposes or for scientific research purposes is intended in the waters of the Gulf of Riga or the Baltic Sea (except for coastal waters), the person shall indicate the following in the submission:

5.1. the name, registration number, address, and telephone number of the recipient of the permit (licence);

5.2. the given name, surname, and telephone number of the representative responsible for the organisation of scientific work or fishing for special purposes;

5.3. the given name, surname, and telephone number of the representative responsible for the fishing activities;

5.4. the given name and surname of the fisherman (fishermen);

5.5. the period during which fishing is intended;

5.6. the location where fishing is intended, indicating the ICES fishing rectangle for the waters of the Gulf of Riga or the Baltic Sea;

5.7. the type, number, or length in metres of the intended fishing gear and the mesh size in millimetres;

5.8. the following information on the vessel (vessels) to be used for fishing:

5.8.1. the name, type, registration number, call sign, port of registry, the name of the owner of the vessel (vessels), and also, if a foreign vessel is used, the owner's address;

5.8.2. the gross tonnage (in tons), total engine power (in kilowatts), speed (in knots), and overall length (in metres);

5.8.3. the given name and surname of the master.

6. A person shall append to the submission a copy of the provisions for use of the body of water or provide a link to the binding regulations of the local government on the website www.likumi.lv where the provisions for use of the body of water are available if fishing is planned in the inland waters or copies of such fisheries research programmes or projects agreed on with the Institute of Food Safety, Animal Health and Environment "BIOR" (hereinafter - the Institute) and the Ministry of Environmental Protection and Regional Development which determine the need to fish for special purposes or for scientific research purposes, and also a document - an explanatory note (if applicable) containing information and justification in respect of the expected deviations from the laws and regulations governing industrial fishing or angling as well as an indication of the amount of catch that is not included in the catch limits set for fishermen.

7. If it is intended to issue multiple permits (licences) based on the documents referred to in Paragraph 6 of this Regulation, the person shall submit copies of the relevant documents only once - for obtaining the first permit (licence).

8. If additional information is required from the person, the Service shall specify the deadline for the submission thereof which is not shorter than five working days.

9. The Service is entitled to refuse to issue the permit (licence) in the following cases:

9.1. if the submission or the accompanying documents do not contain all the necessary information or false information has been provided;

9.2. if the documents referred to in Paragraph 6 of this Regulation which justify the need to fish for special purposes or for scientific research purposes do not accompany the submission.

10. The Service shall take the decision to issue the permit (licence) or to refuse to issue the permit (licence) within 10 working days from the day of receipt of the submission.

11. After taking the decision referred to in Paragraph 10 of this Regulation, the Service shall immediately enter the data on the issued permits (licences), including the personal data referred to in Paragraphs 4 and 5 of this Regulation, into the State information system Latvian Fisheries Integrated Control and Information System (hereinafter - the Information System).

12. The permit (licence) for fishing in the inland waters, the coastal waters of the Gulf of Riga or the Baltic Sea shall be issued in accordance with the sample form of the permit (licence) specified in the Annex to this Regulation. The permit (licence) for fishing in the territorial waters (except for coastal waters) or the economic zone waters of the Republic of Latvia shall be issued in accordance with the sample form of the permit (licence) specified in the laws and regulations regarding industrial fishing in the territorial waters and economic zone waters.

13. If, during the use of the permit (licence), the information provided in the submission or accompanying documents has changed, the person who has obtained the permit (licence) shall inform the Service in writing of the changes within five working days.

14. The Service shall keep the documents and data regarding the issuance of the permit (licence) in paper or electronic form for five years after the expiry of the permit (licence). Data in the Information System shall be kept for 10 years after the expiry of the permit (licence).

15. Cabinet Regulation No. 192 of 15 March 2005, Procedures by which Permits (Licences) for Fishing for Special Purposes and for Scientific Research Purposes shall be Issued (*Latvijas Vēstnesis*, 2005, No. 47; 2008, No. 198; 2009, No. 205; 2012, No. 187; 2013, No. 153), is repealed.
